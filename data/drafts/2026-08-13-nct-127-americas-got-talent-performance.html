NCT 127 will appear on the popular NBC audition program America's Got Talent on August 19 (local time), SM Entertainment announced on the 13th. The group will perform live for American audiences at the Pasadena Civic Auditorium in California.

America's Got Talent, now in its 21st season, is an audition show that discovers talented performers across a wide range of fields including singing, dance, comedy, acrobatics, and magic.

NCT 127 is set to make their comeback on August 24 at 6 p.m. with their 7th full-length album 'BLINGY'. The album includes nine tracks, among them the title track of the same name.

Ahead of the new album's release, the group also ran a large-scale outdoor advertising campaign across 13 cities worldwide from July 24 to August 6. The campaign kicked off in Seoul and extended to New York and Los Angeles in the United States, as well as Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Fuzhou, Chongqing, Chengdu, Hangzhou, Xiamen, Xi'an, and Tianjin in China.
