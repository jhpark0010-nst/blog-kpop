SEVENTEEN's Dino released his first solo mini album 'Gilboard' (吉BOARD) at 6 p.m. on August 3 under his alter ego (sub-character) 'Picheorin,' according to his agency Pledis Entertainment.

'Gilboard' is an album in which Dino interprets music across various genres — including EDM trap, city pop, and new jack swing — through the lens of Korean-style heung (흥), a spirit of joy and exuberance.

The title track 'Nolabose' was produced by renowned composer Hitchhiker, blending a sophisticated and vibrant melody with witty lyrics. The song showcases Picheorin's signature laid-back charm and free-spirited energy.

Dino said through his agency, 'I thought a lot about composing the album with genres that could capture Korea's retro sensibility and the full spectrum of human emotions — joy, anger, sorrow, and pleasure.' He added, 'I tried to express the diverse emotions of life filled with heung and spirit, going beyond simple fun.'

In an unusual move for an idol artist, Dino previewed his new song on KBS 1TV's 'Jeonguk Norae Jarang,' a long-running national singing contest program. He explained, 'As Picheorin, I wanted to approach as many people as possible in a familiar way. After thinking about the best method, this stage was the answer.' He continued, 'When I watch performances by seniors who were active in the 1970s and 80s, I feel a freedom and individuality unique to each artist — I wanted to express myself more freely like that. In keeping with the meaning of the album title 'Gilboard,' I wanted to share the music and the joy, anger, sorrow, and pleasure within it with as many people as possible.'

He also said, '(Through Picheorin) I wanted to challenge a concept I had never tried before. I wanted to show a fresh side that only I can express. I hope 'Gilboard' can bring joy, empathy, and comfort to busy, painful, and exhausting everyday lives — and for myself, I hope it remains as another step of growth and boldness.'
