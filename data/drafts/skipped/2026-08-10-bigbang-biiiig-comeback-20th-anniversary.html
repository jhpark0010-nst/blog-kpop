BIGBANG is making their comeback with a new digital single on August 19, 2026, marking their 20th debut anniversary. On August 10, YG Entertainment revealed the title poster for 'BIGBANG Digital Single [BiiiG]' via their official blog and announced the single will be released at 6 PM KST on August 19.

The unveiled poster features three auras — blue, red, and yellow — cutting through the darkness, combined with the members' silhouettes and sleek 'BiiiG' typography, projecting BIGBANG's signature commanding presence.

The previously unrevealed song title 'BiiiG' was also officially disclosed for the first time. The title is inspired by the word 'BIG,' meaning large or grand, and is closely tied to the group's identity as BIGBANG. The playful spelling twist on a familiar word drew attention and heightened anticipation for the expanded musical world and scale BIGBANG will deliver through 'BiiiG.'

YG Entertainment stated, "'BiiiG' is the song that most vividly captures where BIGBANG stands today, on their 20th debut anniversary." They added, "You can look forward to yet another musical spectrum from BIGBANG, who have built their own unrivaled musical world over many years."

This marks BIGBANG's first new music in approximately 4 years and 4 months since the digital single 'Spring Summer Fall Winter (Still Life),' released in April 2022. At the time, BIGBANG swept major domestic and international music charts without any separate broadcast promotions and won the Digital Music Award at the 37th Golden Disc Awards, proving their enduring power. BIGBANG has previously produced numerous mega-hit songs including 'Lies,' 'Last Farewell,' 'Haru Haru,' 'FANTASTIC BABY,' 'LOSER,' 'BANG BANG BANG,' and 'BAE BAE,' laying the groundwork for the global K-pop wave through their unrivaled musicality, producing ability, and cultural influence spanning fashion and performance.

Meanwhile, BIGBANG will also be launching their 20th anniversary world tour, 'BIGBANG 2026-2027 WORLD TOUR XX : COSMOS,' to coincide with the release of 'BiiiG.' Starting with a performance in Goyang on August 21, the tour will span 19 cities across North America, Europe, Oceania, and Asia for a total of 33 shows, with additional venues to be announced sequentially.
