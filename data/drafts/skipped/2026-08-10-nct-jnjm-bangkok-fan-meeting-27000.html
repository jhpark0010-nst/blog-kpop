NCT JNJM, the unit consisting of NCT members Jeno and Jaemin, drew 27,000 fans at their fan meeting held at Impact Arena in Bangkok, Thailand from August 7 to 9, SM Entertainment announced on the 10th.

NCT JNJM performed a variety of stages including their unit debut track 'BOTH SIDES' and 'WIND UP', among others.

With the Bangkok shows, the duo wrapped up their fan meeting tour, which spanned 6 regions across Asia — including Tokyo, Japan and Jakarta, Indonesia — for a total of 13 performances.

The members shared their thoughts, saying, 'Every moment of the unit fan meeting tour was precious and happy.' They added, 'We hope our charm and energy came through well.'

EXO member and actor D.O. (Do Kyung-soo) will hold a solo concert titled 'DAY OFF' at Ticketlink Live Arena in Songpa-gu, Seoul from October 2 to 4, according to Blitzway Music. This marks his first solo concert in 10 months since December of last year. The concert title 'DAY OFF' carries the meaning of 'taking a rest today, breaking free from the usual routine, and enjoying a new day.' D.O. recently completed a world tour as an EXO member, performing 27 shows across 14 regions worldwide.

Group NOWIEZ held a comeback busking event at COEX in Gangnam-gu, Seoul on the 9th, Cube Entertainment announced. The busking was held to celebrate the release of their digital single 'Achilles', which dropped on the 5th. They performed a variety of stages including the new track 'Achilles', as well as 'EVERGLOW' and 'Problem Child'. NOWIEZ said, 'We were able to enjoy the stage because so many people came to the busking. Thank you for coming out despite the hot weather and on a weekend.' They added, 'We hope those who saw us for the first time will continue to show NOWIEZ a lot of love going forward.'
