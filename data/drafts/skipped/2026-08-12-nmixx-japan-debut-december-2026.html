NMIXX has successfully wrapped up the Asia leg of their first world tour with a concert in Tokyo, and announced their official Japan debut this coming December. The group confirmed that a debut best album titled 'N=MIXX' will be released on December 9.

Starting in Bangkok in June, NMIXX held a total of 8 concerts across 5 Asian cities — Bangkok, Singapore, Kaohsiung, Hong Kong, and Tokyo — as part of their first world tour 'EPISODE 1: ZERO FRONTIER'. In Kaohsiung and Hong Kong, the shows sold out completely, leading to one additional concert date in each city, demonstrating explosive local popularity.

On August 8 and 9, NMIXX performed a standalone concert at Keio Arena in Tokyo, Japan, delivering an intense live performance of 28 songs backed by a full band sound. The setlist ranged from their debut track 'O.O' to 'Phoenix', 'Run for Roses', and 'Soñar (Breaker)'. Member Bae also won over local fans with her wit and fan service, including a vocal cover performance she learned from NiziU's Rima.

Ahead of their official Japan debut in December, the group unveiled the Japanese-language version of 'Blue Valentine' for the first time, drawing an explosive response from the audience.

Leader Haewon and the members shared, 'We will release our Japan debut best album N=MIXX on December 9 and finally make our official debut.' They added, 'Please look forward to the tracks sung in Japanese as well. We will continue to create more opportunities to meet our fans going forward.'
