BIGBANG released a music video teaser for their new song 'BiiiG' on August 18, 2026 — timed to coincide with member G-Dragon's birthday. YG Entertainment posted the 'BIGBANG - BiiiG M/V TEASER' across official social media channels on the same day. The fact that the teaser dropped on G-Dragon's birthday made it an especially meaningful gift for global fans.

The teaser video is set against a dark and distinctive party backdrop, combining the rough texture of a vintage camcorder aesthetic with dramatic lighting contrasts to create a sensory retro mood. Within that setting, G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung captivated viewers with their signature free-spirited energy, playful movements, and personality-filled performances.

The newly revealed lyrics drew particular attention: 'Fan zone Bangbong shining Hee (熙) / Everything big big big big.' The lines showcase BIGBANG's confident pride as they mark their 20th debut anniversary, while also directly weaving in 'Bangbong' — the official light stick of their fandom V.I.P — as a tribute to fans who have stood by them through the years.

BIGBANG is set to officially release their new digital single 'BiiiG' at 6 PM on August 19, 2026, the exact date of their 20th debut anniversary.
