NMIXX wrapped up the Asia leg of their first world tour in Tokyo and shared exciting news: the group will make their official Japan debut with an album release. The six-member group — Lily, Haewon, Sullyoon, BAE, Jiwoo, and Kyujin — completed 8 shows across 5 Asian cities as part of their world tour 'EPISODE 1: ZERO FRONTIER', starting in Bangkok on June 13, followed by Singapore on June 20, Kaohsiung on July 12 and 13, Hong Kong on July 25 and 26, and Tokyo on August 8 and 9. Tickets sold out rapidly in Kaohsiung and Hong Kong, prompting one additional show in each city.

At their solo concerts held on August 8 and 9 at Keio Arena in Tokyo, Japan, NMIXX delivered a live performance of 28 songs to a vibrant band sound, ranging from their debut track 'O.O' to 'Phoenix', 'Run for Roses', and 'Soñar (Breaker)'. The audience cheered enthusiastically, energizing the group throughout the shows.

Leader Haewon welcomed the crowd, saying, 'NSWER (fandom name)! Welcome to our first world tour.' Lily, Sullyoon, BAE, Jiwoo, and Kyujin each introduced their own charms and greeted fans warmly. BAE shared that she had learned from Rima, a member of fellow JYP Entertainment girl group NiziU, and performed the popular song 'Sukisugite Metsu' by local artist M!LK, creating a fun moment of connection with the audience.

The members performed 'Shape of Love' with sweet vocals, showcased a high-difficulty pair choreography by BAE and Kyujin in 'High Horse', and delivered powerful performances of 'SPINNIN' ON IT', 'KNOW ABOUT ME', and 'Red light sign, but we go' with laser light production. They also gave the world premiere of the Japanese-language version of 'Blue Valentine' ahead of their upcoming Japan debut, delighting fans in attendance.

The members said: 'How did you feel about hearing the Japanese version of Blue Valentine for the first time? We will be releasing our Japan debut best album N=MIXX on December 9 and finally making our debut. It will include songs sung in Japanese, so please look forward to it. We want to keep creating many more opportunities to meet all of you.' They expressed their joy about the Japan debut and excitement for future activities.

NMIXX's first world tour kicked off with a sold-out show at Inspire Arena in Incheon in November of last year, and spanned approximately 9 months across 17 cities and 21 shows through Europe, North America, and Asia. Four years after their debut, NMIXX will officially introduce themselves to Japan through the 'NMIXX JAPAN DEBUT SHOWCASE N=MIXX', scheduled for November 7 and 8 in Tokyo and December 12 and 13 in Nagoya.
