Epik High drew attention on July 30, 2026 after gifting Tom Holland a CD from Korean singer Geomi during a meeting in London — a witty nod to the fact that 'geomi' (거미) is the Korean word for 'spider.'

A video titled 'Spider-Man recommends lunch ft. Tom Holland, Zendaya, Jacob Batalon' was uploaded to the Epik High YouTube channel on July 30. The video shows Epik High members meeting the stars of the film 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' — Tom Holland, Zendaya, and Jacob Batalon — in London, where they shared a lively conversation.

Toward the end of the interview, Tablo introduced the gift by saying, 'This is a bit of a funny present.' He explained, 'There is a singer in Korea called Geomi. The Korean word for spider is geomi, and that is actually her real stage name.' He then handed over Geomi's CD.

Upon hearing the explanation, Tom Holland and Zendaya widened their eyes and exclaimed, 'Is that real?' Tom Holland then expressed his gratitude, saying, 'Thank you so much. I now have a CD.'

Viewers who watched the video responded enthusiastically, with comments such as 'Gifting Spider-Man a Geomi CD is such a clever idea,' 'This is genuinely such a fun interview,' and 'It has been a long time since I saw such a lively and enjoyable interview.'

Meanwhile, 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day,' which opened on July 29, is an action blockbuster following Peter Parker (Tom Holland) — who was erased from everyone's memory by Doctor Strange's spell after the events of 'Spider-Man: No Way Home' — as he faces a mysterious enemy who knows his true identity.
