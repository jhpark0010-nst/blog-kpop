To mark the 20th anniversary of BIGBANG's debut, a media exhibition titled 'COSMOS' will be held in Seoul. The exhibition is part of a series of anniversary projects organized to celebrate two decades since the group's debut.

The exhibition is being planned alongside a fan event on August 19 at the Han River and the world tour 'XX : COSMOS,' which opens in Goyang from August 21 to 23, as part of the 20th debut anniversary commemorative project.

Organizer Creative Meot announced on August 4: 'BIGBANG plans to present the music and time they have written over the past 20 years, along with the memories created together with fans, through a new form of media art exhibition that incorporates cutting-edge entertainment technology.'

A prequel (PREQUEL) exhibition will be held at Lotte World Tower in Jamsil, Seoul, followed by the main exhibition at 'City Hall Station Funstation — dududo SEOUL,' an underground idle space between City Hall Station and Euljiro 1-ga Station in Seoul. Specific exhibition dates will be announced at a later time.

Visitors will be able to experience an immersive media exhibition incorporating advanced technologies including AI (artificial intelligence), real-time holograms, XR (extended reality), AR (augmented reality), and next-generation 3D motion graphics.

The exhibition title 'COSMOS' carries a cosmic concept encompassing connection, relationships, and continuity, meaning 'everything that exists in the past, present, and will exist in the future.' The exhibition is a collaboration between organizer YG Entertainment and Creative Meot. Creative Meot previously held a solo media exhibition titled 'Ubermensch' for member G-Dragon across 11 cities worldwide, drawing an audience of 250,000 visitors.
