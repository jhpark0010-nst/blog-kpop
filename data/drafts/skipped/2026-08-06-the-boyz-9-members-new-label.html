THE BOYZ are set for a fresh start as a 9-member group after an ongoing exclusive contract dispute with their agency. According to THE BOYZ representatives on August 6, the nine members — Sangyeon, Jacob, Younghoon, Hyunjae, Juyeon, Kevin, Q, Sunwoo, and Eric — have recently signed a contract for group activities. They are currently preparing to resume full team activities based at a newly established label.

The members released a statement saying: 'From the very beginning, our hearts have never changed — all nine of us must be together. More than anything, we wanted to first convey our intentions to the fans who have been waiting and worrying about THE BOYZ's group activities.'

They continued: 'While doing our best in our individual roles, we will also do our utmost to continue the precious time we have built with our fans as THE BOYZ. We sincerely thank all the THE B who have sent us unwavering love and support. We will prepare hard so that we can repay you with a great showing, so please cheer us on.'

Going forward, the group will operate on a 'separately, yet together' basis. Each member will pursue individual activities through separate contracts with outside agencies best suited to maximize their individual talents. For group activities, however, all nine will maintain a fully cooperative structure and carry on without wavering. Member New, who has chosen to remain with the original agency Cre.Ker Entertainment (One Hundred), will not be participating in group activities.

Earlier, the nine members notified Cre.Ker Entertainment of their exclusive contract termination in February, citing unpaid settlement funds, failure to provide settlement data, and a breakdown of trust due to insufficient management support. In April, the court accepted the members' injunction application to suspend the validity of their exclusive contracts, ruling in their favor. Even amid the dispute, the members demonstrated a strong will to keep the team together, going so far as to fund a concert out of their own pockets.

The legal battle between both sides is still ongoing. Last month, Cre.Ker Entertainment filed for a provisional seizure on real estate under the names of Younghoon and Q, which the court accepted, and the two members immediately filed an objection. Cha Ga-won, chairman of Cre.Ker Entertainment, was arrested on August 4 on charges of fraud under the Act on the Aggravated Punishment of Specific Economic Crimes.
