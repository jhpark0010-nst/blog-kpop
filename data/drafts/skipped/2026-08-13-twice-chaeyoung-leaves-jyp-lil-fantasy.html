TWICE member Chaeyoung announced on August 13 that she is leaving JYP Entertainment, following fellow member Jeongyeon who departed earlier during the group's re-signing season.

Chaeyoung shared the news on her personal SNS, writing: 'I am going to build my music activities in my own LIL FANTASY world. I want to express my gratitude to the people at JYP and the TWICE team for supporting and believing in this wild one for 14 years.'

She continued: 'I also want to say thank you and I love you to our members who have cheered me on and been my strength by my side. And above all, I want to say that my TWICE activities will continue unchanged. I believe the reason I have been loved like this is because of the time I have spent active as TWICE, my roots.'

She added: 'I hope that both Chaeyoung as TWICE and Chaeyoung of LIL FANTASY can become someone who gives you precious music in your lives going forward. Please keep watching over me. I love you.'

TWICE has recently entered a re-signing season. Jeongyeon was the first to leave JYP, signing with Baro Entertainment, where her older sister works. Chaeyoung has now followed Jeongyeon in departing JYP and has promised a new beginning.
