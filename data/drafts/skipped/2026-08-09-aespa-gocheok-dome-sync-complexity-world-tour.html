aespa made their Gocheok Sky Dome debut on August 7 and 8, 2026, drawing a combined audience of 35,000 fans over two nights as part of their fourth world tour, 'Sync: Complexity.' The seats filled all the way up to the fourth floor of the dome despite temperatures well above 30°C. TWICE member Sana and (G)I-DLE member Miyeon were also spotted in attendance.

On the second night, the members expressed their emotions, saying they were 'happy to have held the concert over two days without anyone getting hurt' and thanking their fandom, MY, for filling the venue. Winter reflected on the milestone: 'I never imagined we would be able to perform at Gocheok Dome and fill the seats. We will keep working hard and show you how much we've grown, so please look forward to it.'

Now in their seventh year, aespa drew on experience from three previous world tours and major festival appearances — including Lollapalooza Chicago on August 2 (local time) — to deliver powerful performances and stable live vocals across 27 songs. The setlist opened with 'Lemonade,' released in May, and roared to life when the intros to signature tracks 'Whiplash' and debut song 'Black Mamba' rang out. The group also showcased their vocal chops on album tracks such as 'Count On Me' and 'Can't Help Myself' using handheld microphones.

After eight songs showcasing aespa's signature sharp, metallic performance style, Karina told the crowd: 'Thank you for spending time with us in this hot weather.' True to their global profile, the members communicated with fans in Korean, English, Japanese, and Chinese throughout the show. The setlist also included hit songs 'Armageddon' and 'Next Level,' unit stages, and new solo song reveals.

The unit song 'Lollipop' by Giselle and NingNing created a dreamy atmosphere, while Karina and Winter's 'Serenade' delivered an intense performance. Solo stages highlighted each member's individual color: Karina's R&B dance track '16 Bit' showcased her vocal appeal; Winter transformed into a cowgirl for 'Saddle Up.' Giselle performed 'BYEB4HELLO,' a song she co-wrote, opening with focused live singing before transitioning into a group dance performance. NingNing, who participated in writing 'I Love You But I Gotta Let You Go,' delivered a ballad stage accompanied by violin and cello string arrangements.

As the concert drew to a close, Giselle said: 'We prepared hard for this concert. Because the audience cheered us on so enthusiastically, we were able to enjoy the stage.' NingNing added: 'We will keep showing you better sides of ourselves. I'm excited to meet fans around the world on the world tour.' Karina closed with: 'aespa will always work hard. This concert is an unforgettable memory for us — I hope it is for you too. Thank you always for choosing aespa. We will become an even cooler aespa, so please keep looking forward to us.' Following the Seoul shows, aespa will continue their world tour across 25 regions in North America, South America, and Europe.
