KATSEYE, the global girl group from HYBE and Geffen Records, has set a new personal best on Spotify with their new single 'Animal.' HYBE Labels announced on July 26 that the track achieved the group's highest-ever chart position on the platform on its release day.

'Animal' entered Spotify's Daily Top Songs USA chart at No. 4 as of July 24 (local time). This marks KATSEYE's best-ever ranking on that chart, surpassing their previous hits 'Internet Girl,' which peaked at No. 6, and 'PINKY UP,' which reached No. 10.

On the global stage, 'Animal' debuted at No. 9 on Spotify's Daily Top Songs Global chart. The single also charted in a total of 47 countries and regions, including Canada (No. 7) and the United Kingdom (No. 15), in addition to its No. 4 finish in the United States.

The music video for 'Animal' surpassed 10 million views on YouTube within just one day of its release, making it KATSEYE's fastest music video to reach that milestone. Hollywood actress Demi Moore appears in the video.

KATSEYE is the first girl group debuted in the United States by HYBE and Geffen Records, and has been active on the global stage. In May, the group won three awards at the 2026 American Music Awards (AMAs) — one of the three major U.S. pop music award shows — including New Artist of the Year.
