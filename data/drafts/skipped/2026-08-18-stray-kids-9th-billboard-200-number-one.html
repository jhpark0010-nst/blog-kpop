Stray Kids has achieved their 9th consecutive No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with their new mini album 'THIS & THAT', debuting at the top of the chart as announced by Billboard on August 16 (local time). The group shared their reaction through their agency JYP Entertainment on August 18, saying, 'It feels like fate — in our 9th year since debut, we've achieved our 9th Billboard 200 No. 1.'

The group reflected on the achievement, stating, 'Having earned the record for the most No. 1s among 21st-century groups, we feel a deep sense of meaning as a K-pop group.' This milestone makes Stray Kids the group with the most Billboard 200 No. 1s in the 21st century, the second most in Billboard history after the Beatles (19 times), and ties them with the Rolling Stones.

Stray Kids first topped the Billboard 200 in 2022 with 'ODDINARY', and have since placed eight consecutive albums at No. 1, including '★★★★★', '樂-STAR', 'KARMA', and 'DO IT', continuously breaking their own record for the most No. 1s by a K-pop act. 'THIS & THAT' now extends that streak to nine.

'THIS & THAT' is an album capturing a bold attitude and confidence in pursuing what one wants without being bound by convention. It contains eight tracks, including the title track of the same name.

Stray Kids credited their fans for the achievement, saying, 'Above all, this was made possible thanks to STAY (their fandom name), who have cheered us on without fail.' They added, 'As a team that truly loves music and wants to deliver positive energy and strength to many people through music, we are deeply honored to mark this milestone of nine consecutive Billboard 200 No. 1s.'

The group concluded, 'Just as our team name means finding and forging our own path, we will continue to expand our own music spectrum beyond convention.' They also expressed their wish to 'show the endless growth of Stray Kids and K-pop to music fans around the world, powered by the great love of STAY, who are always by our side.'
