BLACKPINK's Jennie headlined Summer Sonic 2026, one of Japan's largest music festivals, her agency OA Entertainment announced on August 17. She took the stage at two separate dates across the festival run.

Jennie performed on August 14 at Zozomarine Stadium and Makuhari Messe in Tokyo, and on August 16 at Expo '70 Commemorative Park in Osaka.

She served as headliner on both the Tokyo and Osaka dates, leading the shows with signature tracks including 'Mantra' and 'like JENNIE,' as well as unreleased new songs.

This year, Jennie has appeared at a range of global festivals, including The Governors Ball Music Festival in New York, Roskilde Festival in Denmark, Open'er Festival in Poland, Mad Cool Festival 2026 in Spain, Lollapalooza Chicago in the United States, and Summer Sonic 2026 in Japan.
