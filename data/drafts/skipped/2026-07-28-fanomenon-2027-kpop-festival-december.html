FANOMENON 2027, a large-scale K-pop-centered festival billed as Korea's answer to Coachella, will be held for the first time from December 2 to 12, 2027, at Changdong Seoul Arena and the KINTEX Second Exhibition Hall in Goyang. The Popular Culture Exchange Committee (hereinafter 'the Committee') announced the plan at a press briefing held in Jongno-gu, Seoul on the 27th.

The Committee is co-chaired by Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism Choi Hwi-young and JYP Entertainment's chief producer Park Jin-young. The Committee stated that the purpose of the event is 'to bring together the capabilities of K-Culture companies to foster a world-class large-scale event representing Korea, and to spread that growth across tourism, content, and related industries.'

FANOMENON is a privately led large-scale event combining K-pop concerts, a fandom awards ceremony, and exhibition and experience programs from K-Culture companies. The event name is a portmanteau of 'Fan' and 'Phenomenon,' meaning 'a phenomenon created by fans,' reflecting the intent to view fans not merely as consumers but as active partners in spreading K-Culture. A pre-event will be held on December 2, with the main event running from December 3 to 12.

The core programs of FANOMENON are large-scale concerts featuring prominent domestic and international artists, and a fandom-focused awards ceremony. At Changdong Seoul Arena — scheduled for completion in May next year — well-known artists will take the stage in succession over two weeks. The fandom awards ceremony held there will present awards to the fandom that demonstrated the greatest influence over the year, with the artist or brand accepting the award on behalf of the fandom. At KINTEX Second Exhibition Hall in Goyang, festival-style music performances featuring multiple artists will be held alongside K-Culture exhibition and experience events. Companies in food, beauty, fashion, webtoon, film, drama, and animation sectors will participate. Additional side events include fan meetings with singers and actors, production press conferences, video content screenings, game-related performances, e-sports competitions, movie and drama OST concerts, and K-Culture seminars.

The Korean Culture and Tourism Institute projected that FANOMENON will attract a total of 520,000 visitors, including 200,000 international tourists, generating approximately 1 trillion KRW in economic impact. The government plans to actively support key operational areas such as immigration and safety management, and will expand tourism promotion for Korea in conjunction with the event.

Co-chair Choi Hwi-young said, 'FANOMENON is a new attempt to create a massive K-Culture platform where fans around the world can directly participate and experience,' adding, 'We will make it an experience that fans visiting Korea will remember for a long time, and a new opportunity for growth in content, tourism, and related industries.' Co-chair Park Jin-young stated, 'The power of K-Culture comes from the fandom that has grown and spread it together, beyond the artists and content,' and emphasized, 'We will create a successful model that organically combines private creativity and government support, so that Korea can establish itself as the global center of the fandom industry.'
