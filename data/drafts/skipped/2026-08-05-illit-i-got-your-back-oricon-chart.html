ILLIT is continuing its remarkable rise in Japan. According to Oricon on August 5, ILLIT's second Japanese single 'I Got Your Back' sold 71,009 copies in its first week, landing at No. 4 on the latest Weekly Singles Ranking (dated August 10, covering the period July 27 to August 2). This marks ILLIT's highest-ever first-week sales record on the Oricon Weekly Singles Chart.

The single's digital release and music video are also generating strong responses. The title track 'I Got Your Back (Feat. JISOO, MOMOKA of HANA)' topped AWA's real-time trending chart immediately after release, and has since established a stable presence on major streaming platforms including Line Music, Apple Music Japan, and Spotify Japan. The collaboration between ILLIT and JISOO and MOMOKA of group HANA — representing Korea and Japan respectively — is said to be drawing widespread praise from global music fans for its distinctive synergy.

Meanwhile, ILLIT is set to meet local fans through a series of offline events. On August 8, the group will hold a new release commemorative event at Diversity Tokyo Plaza Festival Square, and on August 9 they are scheduled to take the stage at LuckyFes'26, where they are expected to captivate audiences across Japan.
