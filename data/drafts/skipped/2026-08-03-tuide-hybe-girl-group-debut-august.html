New girl group TUIDE will make their official debut on August 24 at 6 p.m. with their first mini album 'TUNE & PLAY', according to their agency ABD. The announcement was made on August 3.

ABD is a new label under HYBE, and TUIDE is the first team to be introduced under it.

TUIDE is a seven-member group consisting of Seohee, Seoyeon, Elena, Jia, Saki, Sea, and Ihaneui. The members come from diverse cultural backgrounds including Korea, the United States, the United Kingdom, and Japan, and were born between 2006 and 2011.

The day before the announcement, TUIDE released photos and videos introducing the members through their official social media accounts. ABD described the group as planning to 'offer a unique kind of enjoyment to global music fans, drawing on each member's distinct personality and their multinational synergy.'

The group name TUIDE is derived from 'Tune the tide,' meaning 'to harmonize the flow.' Master Professional Han Sung-su, who has discovered and developed K-pop stars including SEVENTEEN, After School, IZ*ONE, and TWS, is overseeing the overall production.

The debut album 'TUNE & PLAY' reflects the group's direction: to harmonize different musical languages into a single rhythm (TUNE) and maximize the joy found within that process (PLAY). Ahead of their official debut, TUIDE held a preview event on August 1 and 2 at Layer Studio 11 in Mapo-gu, Seoul, where they performed all the tracks from the album.
