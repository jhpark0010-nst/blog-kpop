Red Velvet released their new mini album 'Velvet Summer' at 6 p.m. on August 3, as announced by SM Entertainment. This marks the group's return as a full five-member unit for the first time in 2 years and 2 months, since their June 2024 release 'Cosmic.' In the intervening period, the members had each been focused on individual activities.

The title track 'Surfin' Boy' is a dance song featuring a refreshing sound and a cool yet dreamy vocal harmony. Member Joy participated in writing the lyrics for the track.

Irene shared her feelings about the comeback, saying, 'I'm so grateful to our ReVeluvs (fandom name) who waited for over two years.' She added, 'Getting together after such a long time, I love the noisy practice room — I love everything about it.'

Wendy said, 'Preparing an album as a full group after such a long time, I felt truly excited.' She continued, 'I want to repay everyone who waited with great music and performances.'

Seulgi also commented, 'Coming together after each of our solo activities, I feel like we've each grown a lot individually.' She added, 'We worked on this hoping that our own sense of maturity would come through in the album, so please look forward to it.'
