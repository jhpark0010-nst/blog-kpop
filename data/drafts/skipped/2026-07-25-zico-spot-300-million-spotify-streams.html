ZICO's hit track 'SPOT!' (featuring Jennie of BLACKPINK) has surpassed 300 million streams on Spotify, the world's largest music streaming platform. His agency KOZ Entertainment announced the milestone on July 25. This marks the first time any of ZICO's songs has crossed the 300 million cumulative play count on Spotify.

'SPOT!' is a song capturing an unfiltered moment between two friends who happen to meet at a late-night party. The track drew significant attention for featuring BLACKPINK's Jennie. ZICO is also set to hold a solo concert at Asia World-Expo Hall 7 in Hong Kong on September 25.

Girl group aespa's first Japanese mini-album 'KISS N TELL' topped the Oricon daily album chart dated July 23, according to their agency SM Entertainment. The album contains a total of six tracks, including the house-based dance-pop title track of the same name, 'Orbit Pop' — which carries a message about charting one's own path forward — and 'Done with Rules,' which evokes a Y2K atmosphere.

aespa will kick off their new world tour 'SYNK : COMPLaeXITY' at Gocheok Sky Dome in Seoul on August 7 and 8, before heading to North America, South America, and Europe.

Group CORTIS will live-screen their solo concert at YouTube Theater in Los Angeles on August 13 (local time) across more than 870 theater screens in 29 countries worldwide, their agency Big Hit Music announced. The screening will take the form of a 'live viewing' event, where audiences gather in one place to watch a concert together. Some regions will receive a delayed broadcast to account for time differences.

Big Hit Music stated, 'Audiences can enjoy CORTIS's passionate performance on a large screen with rich sound,' and added that 30 minutes of exclusively produced content will be shown before the concert begins. In Los Angeles, a 'Live Viewing Party' will also be held at Vermont Hollywood, a multipurpose performance venue, in addition to the theater screenings.
