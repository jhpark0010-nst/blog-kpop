Hearts2Hearts members Jiwoo, Juun, and Ye-on issued official apologies on August 14 over a racial controversy that arose from an Afro-style wig worn during a Japanese variety program. Each member posted separate statements in both Korean and English on the fan communication platform Weverse.

The controversy originated from a variety show titled 'Congrats! Hearts2Hearts' Owarai Dojo for Their Japan Debut,' which aired on August 12 on the Japanese OTT platform ABEMA. During the broadcast, the members wore Afro-style wigs as props while performing a Michael Jackson routine as part of a comedy mission. Some international fans criticized the segment as mockery of a specific race and culture.

Jiwoo said, 'While filming, I wore the wig without really stopping to think about what it represented or how hurtful it would be. I've been listening and learning from what everyone has shared, and I understand why it was wrong. I'm truly sorry. I, along with all of the members, will be more thoughtful and mindful moving forward.'

Juun stated, 'I've taken some time to reflect on my actions, and I realize that I should have been more thoughtful and careful in that moment. I never intended to hurt or disrespect anyone, but I understand that my actions may have caused hurt, and I'm truly sorry. I've learned from this experience, and I'll be more thoughtful and mindful of my actions going forward.'

Ye-on added, 'Through this, I've had a chance to reflect and learn more about the impact my actions can have, even when there was no intention to hurt anyone. I understand that I need to be more thoughtful and aware of how my actions may affect others. I'm truly sorry to anyone I may have hurt or disappointed, and I'll be more mindful and responsible moving forward.'
