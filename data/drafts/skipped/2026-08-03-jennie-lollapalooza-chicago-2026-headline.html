BLACKPINK's Jennie delivered an overwhelming performance at Lollapalooza Chicago, taking the stage as a headliner on August 1 (local time). She closed out the festival's main Bud Light stage, drawing explosive cheers from the massive crowd gathered at one of North America's biggest music events.

Lollapalooza, which began in 1991, features 8 stages, over 170 artists, and draws more than 400,000 attendees, making it the largest festival of its kind in North America. Jennie joined fellow headliners Charli XCX, Lorde, and Olivia Dean, drawing enormous attention and anticipation from fans around the world.

Most notably, Jennie became the first Korean female solo artist to headline Lollapalooza, and further cemented her status by serving as the closing headliner of the main stage. The Bud Light stage has previously hosted global icons such as The Cure, Coldplay, and The Weeknd, and Jennie stood alongside them as a top-tier global artist.

Over the course of approximately one hour, Jennie performed around 18 songs across various genres with a live band, delivering a flawless show. She filled the stage on her own, presenting freshly rearranged versions of her hit songs to heighten the atmosphere, and actively engaged with the audience to bring the festival to a spectacular close.

The concert marked Jennie's first live performance following the official release of her new single 'Less than a Lover,' and the crowd's passionate sing-alongs confirmed the track's strong global reception. Adding to the excitement, Jennie surprised the audience mid-show by performing a previously unreleased new song, sending the crowd into an enormous frenzy and raising anticipation for her upcoming release.

Meanwhile, Jennie is set to continue performing at major festivals, including SUMMER SONIC 2026 in Japan.
