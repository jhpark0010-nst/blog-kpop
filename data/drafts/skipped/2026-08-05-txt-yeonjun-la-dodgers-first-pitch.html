TOMORROW X TOGETHER (TXT) member Yeonjun is set to take the mound at a Major League Baseball (MLB) stadium as a ceremonial first pitch thrower. On August 13 (local time), Yeonjun will throw the first pitch at Dodger Stadium in Los Angeles ahead of the game between the LA Dodgers and the Milwaukee Brewers.

The venue previously drew attention when BTS's V and soccer player Son Heung-min threw ceremonial first pitches there last year. Yeonjun's appearance on the same mound at the Dodgers' home stadium further confirms his growing global influence.

Yeonjun will also participate in 'Korean Heritage Night,' held in and around Dodger Stadium on the same day. This is a signature cultural promotion that the LA Dodgers have continued annually, designed to share Korean culture with local fans and foster exchange with the Korean-American community. Yeonjun plans to delight the audience with a standout performance.

Having recently made a strong impact with his second mini album 'NO LABELS: PART 02' and its title track 'Ice Cream,' Yeonjun is steadily expanding his activities in the United States across broadcasting, live performances, and sporting events. On August 5, he appears on ABC's flagship morning news program Good Morning America for its Summer Concert Series — only the second Korean solo artist to do so, after BTS's Jungkook. On August 6, he received the honor of being the first Korean artist invited to Z100's Summer Bash Presented By Wells Fargo, hosted by iHeartMedia's leading radio station Z100. On August 12, he is also set to perform at Billboard: One Night Only, a special live event organized by Billboard. He is expected to connect with local fans across broadcasting, live stages, radio, and digital platforms.

Meanwhile, TXT — the group Yeonjun belongs to — will perform at KCON LA 2026, held from August 14 to 16 at the LA Convention Center and Crypto.com Arena. All five members will take the stage as headliners on the final day, August 16, while Yeonjun will deliver a solo performance on August 15.
