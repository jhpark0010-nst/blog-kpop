SEVENTEEN members Seungkwan and Dino will enlist in the military together on October 26, 2026. Fellow member Mingyu will begin alternative service (social service worker) starting September 10.

SEVENTEEN's agency, Pledis Entertainment, announced on August 10 via the fan communication platform Weverse: 'Seungkwan and Dino are scheduled to enlist in the Army as military band soldiers on October 26.' The agency added, 'As the enlistment ceremony at the training center is a gathering that includes many soldiers and their families, please refrain from visiting in person.'

Pledis continued: 'Mingyu will fulfill his mandatory military service obligation beginning September 10.' The agency also stated, 'There will be no separate official events on the start date of service or on the day of training center entry, and please refrain from visiting his alternative service workplace.'

The three members are expected to participate in all scheduled activities without disruption prior to their enlistment dates.

With this, all SEVENTEEN members subject to mandatory military service will have fulfilled their obligations — excluding S.Coups, who received a military exemption, Chinese members The8 and Jun, and American member Joshua.
