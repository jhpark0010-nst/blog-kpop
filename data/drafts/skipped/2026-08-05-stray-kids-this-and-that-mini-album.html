Stray Kids have shared a behind-the-scenes look at their new mini album ahead of its release. JYP Entertainment posted an intro video titled 'Stray Kids [INTRO "THIS & THAT"]' on their official account on August 4, featuring members explaining the new album in their own words.

Stray Kids will release their mini album 'THIS & THAT' and its title track of the same name on August 7 at 1 PM KST. The group's in-house producing team 3RACHA — Bang Chan, Changbin, and Han — participated in producing all tracks on the album.

The group described the album as one they 'prepared without any regrets' and 'an album full of new attempts.' They named the title track 'This & That' as the song they spent the most time perfecting. The members said they 'completed the Stray Kids identity with a sound that feels different from previous title tracks — popular yet fresh — along with catchy lyrics, wit, and performance.'

Changbin explained the lyric-writing process, saying he 'put a lot of thought and effort into finding a hip word to express "this" and "that."' The team also revealed details about the music video, which carries a Korean aesthetic, as well as the choreography, which went through multiple rounds of revisions.

Stray Kids previously set a record of eight consecutive No. 1 entries on the Billboard 200 with their album 'DO IT,' released in November of last year. This new release marks their return approximately nine months later.

Stray Kids are an eight-member multinational boy group under JYP Entertainment who debuted on March 25, 2018. Their name means 'wandering children,' reflecting the idea of eight members breaking free from convention and forging their own path together.
