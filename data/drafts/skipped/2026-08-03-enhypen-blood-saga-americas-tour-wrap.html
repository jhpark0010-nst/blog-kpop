ENHYPEN has wrapped up their North and South America tour 'BLOOD SAGA,' drawing a total of 193,000 fans across 12 performances in 8 cities. Belift Lab announced the figures on August 3. The tour concluded on August 1 (local time) with a performance at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas, USA.

At the end of the concert, ENHYPEN expressed their feelings, saying, 'We are grateful to ENGENE (the fandom name) for giving us so much love, passion, and memories.' They added, 'We will return to Korea and work hard to prepare our upcoming 8th mini album, THE SIN : BLISS. We will come back with even better music and more amazing performances, so let's meet again soon.'

ENHYPEN is set to hold concerts at Busan Sajik Indoor Gymnasium on August 8 and 9.

Singer Kim Jaejoong will release a new single, 'THE WAVE,' on August 20, according to Encode Entertainment. This marks his first new release in one year and three months since his mini album 'Beauty in Chaos,' which came out in May of last year. He will also hold a solo concert at KBS Arena in Gangseo-gu, Seoul on August 29 and 30.

BOYNEXTDOOR will release their second Japanese digital single on August 18, KOZ Entertainment announced. Following the Japanese single release, they will hold performances for their world tour 'KNOCK ON Vol.2' at K-Arena Yokohama in Kanagawa from August 21 to 23, with the tour continuing in Saga, Osaka, and Nagano. On September 28, they will make a comeback with the repackage of their first full-length album.
