Japanese group JO1 has cancelled their upcoming Seoul concerts scheduled for September 26 and 27 at the Handball Gymnasium (Ticketlink Live Arena) in Songpa-gu, Seoul. The cancellation comes as a result of prolonged protests near the venue that have disrupted events in the area.

JO1's label LAPONE Entertainment announced on August 3 via their official website: 'The Seoul performances have been unavoidably cancelled.' The label explained that after comprehensively reviewing the situation around the venue and all operational requirements, they determined it would be 'extremely difficult to proceed with the scheduled performances as planned.'

LAPONE added, 'We have explored various options from multiple angles, including changing the venue, in order to hold a safe concert, and have repeated careful discussions.' The label also stated, 'We sincerely apologize to the fans who have been eagerly looking forward to this concert.'

JO1 is a Japan-based group created by CJ ENM, and had been set to meet their Korean fans through this concert.

In recent months, a prolonged 'ballot box blockade protest' near the Handball Gymnasium has led to a series of concert cancellations and venue changes across the music industry. The protests have continued since a ballot shortage incident during the nationwide local elections held on June 3.

As a result, singer Park Seo-jin's concert scheduled last month was cancelled, while TVXQ's U-Know Yunho and band N.Flying relocated their concerts to Jamsil Indoor Stadium.
