BLACKPINK member Jennie has reached No. 1 on a Billboard radio chart that K-pop artists have historically struggled to crack. Her collaboration with Tame Impala, 'Dracula', topped the Billboard Adult Pop Airplay chart for the week, according to a report by Forbes on July 27 (local time).

The Adult Pop Airplay chart was introduced by Billboard in 1996 and is calculated based on the number of times a song is broadcast across 40 adult-format radio stations in the United States.

While countless K-pop songs have reached the upper tiers of major charts such as the Hot 100 and Billboard 200, the genre has notably struggled to make a significant impact on U.S. radio airplay rankings. This is because digital sales, album sales, and streaming counts can be driven by concentrated fandom support, whereas radio airplay requires broad mainstream popularity across diverse segments of the American public.

Among K-pop-related songs, the only previous track to reach No. 1 on the Adult Pop Airplay chart was 'Golden', the theme song from the Netflix animated film 'K-Pop Demon Hunters'. Sung by Korean-American singer EJAE, Rei Ami, and Audrey Nuna, 'Golden' topped the chart in January of this year, boosted by the wide popularity of the animated film.

'Dracula' is now the second K-pop-related song to reach No. 1 on this chart, following 'Golden'. The track was originally released in October of last year as a solo song by Australian artist Tame Impala. A remix version featuring Jennie was released in February of this year and went viral on short-form video platforms, sending the song back up the charts.

The song is currently sitting at No. 1 across seven Billboard categories: Pop Airplay, Hot Rock & Alternative Songs, Hot Rock Songs, Hot Alternative Songs, Hot Dance/Electronic Songs, and Dance Streaming Songs.
