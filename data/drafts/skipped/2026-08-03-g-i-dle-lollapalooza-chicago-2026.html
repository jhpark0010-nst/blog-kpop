(G)I-DLE dominated the Lollapalooza Chicago stage with an overwhelming command of the crowd. The group — Miyeon, Minnie, Soyeon, Yuqi, and Shuhua — took the T-Mobile Stage at Grant Park in Chicago on July 31 (local time) and delivered an approximately 60-minute performance at the Lollapalooza festival.

Greeted by the roaring cheers of local fans, (G)I-DLE opened the show with a newly arranged, band-sound version of 'Super Lady.' They went on to perform mega-hits including 'TOMBOY,' 'Queencard,' and 'Fate,' as well as 'Gimme Dat Love' and 'Crow' from their 9th mini album 'We made,' released in July — showcasing a total of 16 songs with a live band arrangement and proving their reputation as a performance-driven idol group.

The stage also featured the world premiere performance of 'Morning,' another track from the 9th mini album 'We made,' which drew an enthusiastic response. 'Morning' has recently risen to No. 1 on the Korean YouTube daily Shorts trending chart, drawing attention as (G)I-DLE's newest hit to gain renewed popularity.

The members led their first-ever Lollapalooza stage with ease, even amid bad weather, demonstrating polished English skills and refined stage presence. Soyeon addressed the crowd in both Korean and English, saying, 'Wherever you are, whoever you are, I hope everyone can come together and enjoy this music.' The group then closed the show with 'Mono (Feat. skaiwater),' a song carrying a message about what truly matters.

(G)I-DLE is set to wrap up their world tour with the '2026 i-dle WORLD TOUR [Syncopation] IN MACAU,' scheduled for August 29 and 30 at Galaxy Arena in Macau.
