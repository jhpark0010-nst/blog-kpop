aespa will broadcast their first-ever Gocheok Sky Dome concert to fans around the world through both in-theater live viewings and online streaming. The concerts, part of the '2026-27 aespa LIVE TOUR - SYNK : COMPLaeXITY,' are scheduled to take place on August 7 and 8 at Gocheok Sky Dome in Seoul.

This concert marks aespa's first performance at Gocheok Sky Dome and serves as the opening stage of their new world tour.

The August 7 show will be screened as a live viewing event at 146 theaters across 11 regions, including South Korea. The August 8 show will be broadcast online via Beyond LIVE and Weverse.

Starting with the August 7-8 Seoul Gocheok Dome concerts, aespa will embark on a new world tour spanning a total of 25 cities across North America, South America, and Europe.
