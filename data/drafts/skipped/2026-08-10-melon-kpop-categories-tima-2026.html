Two new Melon-linked award categories will be introduced at the 2026 TMElive International Music Awards (TIMA), a major music awards show organized by China's Tencent Music. The event is scheduled to take place on August 22–23 at Kai Tak Stadium in Hong Kong.

Kakao Entertainment, the operator of the Korean music streaming platform Melon, announced on August 10 that the 'Global-K Artist' and 'Melon Music Choice' categories will be added to this year's TIMA.

TIMA is a large-scale music event that combines an awards ceremony with live performances. This year marks its second edition, following last year's inaugural show. The 2026 lineup features 28 K-pop acts, including Super Junior, Stray Kids, TOMORROW X TOGETHER (TXT), aespa, IVE, BABYMONSTER, PLAVE, RIIZE, NCT WISH, and All Day Project.

The 'Global-K Artist' category will determine its winner by combining data from the 'Global-K Chart' — which launched in June of this year — at a 30% weighting, with fan votes accounting for the remaining 70%. The 'Global-K Chart' is an integrated chart that aggregates usage data from the leading music platforms of South Korea, China, and Japan: Melon, Tencent Music, and Line Music respectively.

The 'Melon Music Choice' category targets artists who achieved meaningful results and received widespread love on Melon throughout the year. The winner will be determined entirely through a judging process.

A Kakao Entertainment representative stated, 'The establishment of these new award categories carries great significance in that leading global music platforms are together shining a spotlight on K-pop.' The representative added, 'We will continue our role as a leader in the music industry by connecting K-pop artists and fans more broadly through diverse global partnerships and platform collaborations.'
