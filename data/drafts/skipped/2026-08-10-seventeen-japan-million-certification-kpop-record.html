SEVENTEEN has received a Million certification from the Recording Industry Association of Japan (RIAJ) Gold Disc Awards. Their agency, Pledis Entertainment, announced on August 10 that the group's Japanese best album 'ALWAYS YOURS', released in 2023, surpassed a cumulative shipment of 1 million copies last month, earning the RIAJ Million certification.

This marks SEVENTEEN's fourth career Million certification, following their Japanese mini album 1st 'DREAM', Korean mini album 10th 'FML', and mini album 11th 'SEVENTEENTH HEAVEN'. According to the agency, this is the most Million certifications ever achieved by a K-pop artist.

SEVENTEEN shared their feelings, saying, 'Following the Million certification for SEVENTEENTH HEAVEN last month, we are grateful and honored to receive another piece of wonderful news.' They added, 'We will continue to show you a variety of activities, so please keep cheering us on warmly.'

The RIAJ awards monthly certifications based on cumulative album shipments: Gold (100,000 or more), Platinum (250,000 or more), Double Platinum (500,000 or more), Triple Platinum (750,000 or more), and Million (1,000,000 or more).

In addition, IVE's Japanese mini album 4th 'LUCID DREAM', ATEEZ's Japanese single 5th 'BAD', and NCT WISH's Japanese single 3rd 'YO-I-DON!/BOY MEETS GIRL' each received Platinum certifications.

aespa's Japanese mini album 2nd 'KISS N TELL', ILLIT's Japanese single 2nd 'I Got Your Back', and NiziU's first best album 'Portfolio' — NiziU being a Japan-localized group created by JYP Entertainment — each received Gold certifications.
