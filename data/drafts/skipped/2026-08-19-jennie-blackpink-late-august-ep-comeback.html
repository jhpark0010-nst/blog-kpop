BLACKPINK's Jennie is making a solo comeback. According to her agency OA Entertainment (ODDATELIER) on August 19, Jennie is preparing to release a new EP with a target of late August.

Throughout this year, Jennie has headlined 7 major global festivals in succession, starting with The Governors Ball Music Festival in New York, USA, followed by Roskilde Festival in Denmark, Open'er Festival in Poland, 2026 MAD COOL FESTIVAL in Spain, and SUMMER SONIC 2026 in Japan, among others. With her steady live performances and powerful stage presence, she captivated music fans around the world, proving herself as a true 'Festival Queen.'

At select festival stages, Jennie had been gradually unveiling unreleased tracks, including 'Less than a Lover,' 'FALLEN ANGEL,' and 'lockitdown,' building anticipation for her new release. Having set the festival stages ablaze with these various unreleased songs, she has now confirmed a late August comeback, leaving fans eager to find out which tracks will be included on the new EP.

As Jennie has traveled the global festival circuit previewing new unreleased music, excitement is soaring over what musical world and performances she will showcase through this upcoming release.
