EXO member and actor D.O. (Doh Kyung-soo) is set to embark on a new concert tour. According to his agency Blitzway Music on August 10, D.O. will hold the '2026-2027 DOH KYUNG SOO CONCERT TOUR [DAY OFF] IN SEOUL' at Ticketlink Live Arena in Seoul for three days, from October 2 to 4.

'DAY OFF' is D.O.'s first solo concert in approximately 10 months, following the conclusion of his Asia tour Seoul finale 'DO it! THE FINAL' in December of last year.

The concert carries the message of 'taking a DAY OFF today, breaking free from the usual routine, and enjoying a brand-new day.'

Fans are eager to see what kind of stages and music D.O. will present at this solo concert tour, his first in about 10 months.

D.O. debuted with EXO in 2012. He began his acting career through the 2014 drama 'It's Okay, That's Love,' and expanded into solo music activities with the release of his first mini-album 'Empathy' in 2021. His next project is the drama 'We Are the Zombie,' scheduled to air next year.
