Stray Kids have achieved a triple million-seller milestone with their new mini-album 'THIS & THAT,' surpassing 3 million copies sold in the first week of release. JYP Entertainment announced on August 15 that the group reached this milestone based on Hanteo Chart figures.

Released on August 7, 'THIS & THAT' recorded sales of 3,289,269 copies through the day before the announcement. This makes it the second highest first-week sales figure among all K-pop albums released this year, trailing only BTS's 'ARIRANG.'

This is also the fourth time Stray Kids have achieved first-week sales of 3 million copies or more, following their full-length album '★★★★★' (Five-Star), mini-album '樂-STAR' (ROCK-STAR), and full-length album 'KARMA.'

JYP stated, 'The first-week sales of this album are approximately 1 million copies higher than the previous release 'DO IT,'' adding that it 'proves the expectations for Stray Kids in 2026.'

The title track 'THIS & THAT' reached No. 1 on the Circle Chart weekly download chart. Stray Kids also claimed No. 1 on various music programs with the song, including Mnet's 'M Countdown' and KBS 2TV's 'Music Bank.' The group is also expected to enter the top tier of the Billboard 200 main album chart, results of which will be released early next week.
