BTS Jin's solo track 'Don't Say You Love Me' has surpassed 1 billion streams on Spotify, the world's largest music streaming platform. His label Big Hit Music announced the milestone on August 18, 2026.

'Don't Say You Love Me' is the title track from Jin's second solo mini-album 'Echo,' released in May of last year. The pop song captures the ironic emotions of two lovers at the end of a relationship, unable to easily let go of each other.

After its release, 'Don't Say You Love Me' debuted at No. 90 on the Billboard Hot 100 main singles chart in the United States. It marked the third time Jin had entered the chart as a solo artist, following 'The Astronaut' in 2022 and 'Running Wild' in 2024.

The track also reached No. 2 on Spotify's Weekly Top Songs Global chart, No. 6 on the Billboard Global 200, and No. 4 on the Billboard Global (Excl. U.S.) chart.

BTS, the group Jin belongs to, is currently holding the 'Arirang' world tour, the largest-scale world tour in K-pop history. BTS is set to meet fans at Rogers Stadium in Toronto, Canada on August 22 and 23 (local time).
