BIGBANG is set to hold a media exhibition to mark their 20th debut anniversary. YG Entertainment announced on August 4 that the group will host the media exhibition 'BIGBANG 20TH ANNIVERSARY MEDIA EXHIBITION COSMOS' (hereafter 'COSMOS') in collaboration with global entertainment tech company Creative MUT.

The exhibition is a core piece of the 20th anniversary project, following the Han River fan event (fireworks and drone show) and the world tour 'XX : COSMOS'. It will translate BIGBANG's 20 years of music, time, and memories shared with fans into a new form of media art powered by cutting-edge entertainment technology.

'COSMOS' is structured so that fans can encounter BIGBANG's 20-year journey across various locations in central Seoul — beginning with a prequel exhibition at Lotte World Tower in Jamsil, and continuing to the main exhibition held in underground spaces between City Hall Station and Euljiro 1-ga Station.

The 20th anniversary narrative starts at the prequel exhibition and flows into the main exhibition, offering a multi-dimensional experience of BIGBANG's story as a cultural icon who has led the direction of K-pop for the past two decades.

A teaser poster released alongside the announcement captures BIGBANG's iconic presence through silhouettes of intersecting light and shadow. The 'COSMOS' logo, shaped to represent the infinity of the universe, symbolizes BIGBANG's eternal musical journey and the time they will continue to share with fans, building anticipation for the exhibition.

The prequel exhibition at Lotte World Tower in Jamsil will be free of charge, with reservations opening via Naver on August 7. The main exhibition, to be held at 'City Hall Station FunStation – dududo SEOUL,' will have its ticket opening on August 13 via Naver. Further details will be released gradually through BIGBANG's official fan platform B.stage and the exhibition's official social media account (@cosmos.exh).
