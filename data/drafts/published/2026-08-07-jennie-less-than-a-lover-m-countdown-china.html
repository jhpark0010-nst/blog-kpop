BLACKPINK member Jennie claimed the No. 1 spot on Mnet's M Countdown (엠카운트다운) on August 6 with her new single 'Less than a Lover,' earning her second consecutive week at the top of the music show and her second trophy overall from the program.

'Less than a Lover' has been receiving global love across major streaming platforms. The track charted at No. 11 on the Billboard Global (Excl. U.S.) chart and No. 26 on the Billboard Global 200. It also reached No. 66 on Spotify's Weekly Top Songs Global chart, while appearing in the upper ranks of Spotify's Daily Top Songs chart in South Korea, Malaysia, and numerous other countries and regions.

On Apple Music's Top Songs chart, the single ranked highly in Singapore, Cambodia, Laos, and many other countries and regions. It also reached No. 1 on the iTunes Top Songs chart in 27 countries and regions including Vietnam, Chile, Indonesia, and Peru — the most No. 1 positions among all K-pop solo songs released this year.

The global popularity of 'Less than a Lover' extended to China as well. The track topped all three of China's major music platforms: QQ Music, NetEase Cloud Music, and Kugou Music. Notably, on QQ Music it became the only female solo single released this year to receive a Gold certification, adding a meaningful milestone to its chart run.

The song is also making its presence felt in the domestic South Korean music market. 'Less than a Lover' ranked No. 1 on the Bugs real-time chart, Apple Music's 'Today's TOP 100: South Korea' chart, and YouTube Korea's trending videos chart. It has also consistently placed in the upper ranks of major streaming charts including Melon's TOP 100 and Genie Music's real-time chart.

'Less than a Lover,' which Jennie co-wrote and co-composed, was created with summer in mind. It captures the thrill and lingering feeling of a relationship that sits somewhere between lovers and friends through the phrase 'Less than a Lover.' The alternative pop track layers a dreamy summer-night romance over a languid texture created by lo-fi guitar and a vintage electric keyboard. Meanwhile, after successfully completing her Lollapalooza stage, Jennie is set to continue her global festival run, with her appearance at SUMMER SONIC 2026 in Japan already announced.
