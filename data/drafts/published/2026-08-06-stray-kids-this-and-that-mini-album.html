Stray Kids held a press conference on August 6, 2026 at a hotel in Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul to celebrate the release of their new mini album 'THIS & THAT.' The group declared, 'We worked hard on this album too,' and added, 'Just like the album title, please watch as Stray Kids does this and that — everything.' The album drops on August 7 at 1 p.m.

Leader Bang Chan explained, 'Stray Kids are in their ninth year, and we have been trying various music genres since our debut. Thanks to that, we were able to show many different sides of ourselves. We worked hard to show a new color of Stray Kids with this album.' The new release comes nine months after their previous album 'DO IT,' which was released in November last year.

'THIS & THAT' is an album that captures a bold attitude and confidence — doing whatever one wants without being bound by convention. It contains eight tracks, including the title track 'THIS & THAT,' pre-release single 'RUN IT,' and album tracks 'After You' and 'Geunal.' 3RACHA — the in-group producing unit of Bang Chan, Changbin, and Han — participated in producing all tracks.

Changbin described the title track: 'Unlike songs we have shown before, 'THIS & THAT' is a chill and relaxed side of Stray Kids. It contains our confidence that we can do this and that — everything — just like the album name.' He added, 'From when we were working on the song in the United States, I was convinced it would be the title track. The hook came together quickly and the choreography came to mind. The members liked it too, so I became even more certain.' Han also shared, 'If you keep making the same music every time, it gets uninteresting. The reason I keep challenging myself musically is that novelty is more fun than familiarity. It will definitely be difficult and there will be variables, but you learn more by going through that process.'

Stray Kids have achieved eight consecutive number-one entries on the Billboard 200 with albums from 'ODDINARY' in 2022 through 'KARMA' and 'DO IT,' earning the title of the K-pop act with the most number-one albums on the chart. On the possibility of a ninth consecutive chart-topper, Seungmin said, 'Every time I prepare an album, I always approach it with the mindset of giving my best rather than focusing on results. I hope the right results follow. I hope our music reaches Stay and our listeners.'

At the press conference, a question arose about whether Stray Kids would submit music to the Grammy Awards next year. Recently, BTS had indirectly criticized the newly created 'Best Asian Pop Music Performance' category and announced they would not be submitting music to next year's Grammy Awards. The members responded, 'We will focus on our music,' without giving a specific answer on the Grammy question. Hyunjin emphasized, 'Not once while promoting have I set any award result or record as a goal. The goal for this album is simply to show and prove that Stray Kids is a team that can do everything well.'

The members also spoke openly about the upcoming period of military service. Lino, born in 1998, said, 'I think the duty of national defense is a matter of course. When the country calls, I will faithfully fulfill my duty. We haven't talked in detail about team activity plans after the military hiatus, but what is certain is that we will come back together. I want to repay the love we have received from Stay. Please wait just a little — we'll be back soon.' Changbin closed with the group's outlook: 'Going forward, Stray Kids will deliver messages through songs we make ourselves, seek out what we do well, and also challenge what is unfamiliar, all while having fun with music.'
