KATSEYE, the joint girl group from HYBE and Geffen Records, has debuted at No. 1 on Spotify's Top Song Debuts Global chart (dated July 24–26) with their new single 'Animal,' according to an announcement by HYBE Labels on July 29.

The Top Song Debuts Global chart covers newly released tracks within a given week. During the same chart period, 'Animal' also ranked No. 2 on the Top Song Debuts USA chart.

'Animal' was co-written by pop star Ed Sheeran and is characterized by an upbeat rhythm and an addictive chorus.

The track gained attention after it was used as background music in a promotional video for the film 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day.'

KATSEYE is set to release their third mini-album, WILD, on August 14.
