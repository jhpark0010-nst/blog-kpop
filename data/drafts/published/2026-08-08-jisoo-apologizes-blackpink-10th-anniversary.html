BLACKPINK's Jisoo bowed her head in apology on August 8, 2026 — the group's 10th debut anniversary — posting a message on the fan platform that began: 'I think this anniversary feels like a day filled with a great sense of sorry.'

She continued: 'Over these 10 years, so many things have happened as BLACKPINK and as Jisoo, but I always wanted to show only my best side and be someone who brings happiness in difficult moments. My heart is heavy because so many BLINKs are hurting.' She added: 'But I wanted to say that I have not forgotten that it is BLINKs who have made BLACKPINK shine this brightly.'

Jisoo closed her message with: 'Thank you for celebrating the 10th anniversary, and I am once again sorry for causing such great disappointment. I want to say I am always grateful and that I love you. Your hearts must be very upset, but I hope there is still a small space left inside for a little sincerity to find its way in.'

The apology came amid widespread fan backlash. BLACKPINK had not prepared any event ahead of their 10th debut anniversary, and only after fan frustration peaked did they hastily announce a meet-and-greet just two days before the date — without clearly disclosing the specific venue or time. Only 40 fans were invited to the event, and eligibility was restricted to fan platform membership subscribers who had been largely inactive for over a year, with a 9-hour application window.

The group also announced in advance that not all members might attend, stating that 'only some members available by schedule will participate.' Initially, Lisa was not attending and Jennie's participation was still being discussed, but amid the negative public reaction, both quickly shifted to 'attending.'
