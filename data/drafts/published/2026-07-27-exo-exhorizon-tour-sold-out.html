EXO has successfully concluded their sixth solo concert tour. Starting in Seoul in April, the group held a total of 27 shows across 14 cities — including Ho Chi Minh City, Nagoya, Taipei, Bangkok, Macau, Osaka, Jakarta, Hong Kong, Kuala Lumpur, Manila, Tokyo, Kaohsiung, and Singapore — under the title 'EXO PLANET #6 - EXhOrizon.' Every single show on the tour sold out, and additional performances as well as restricted-view seats were opened in some cities to meet demand.

Under a production concept built around the group's lore and universe, EXO delivered a wide range of stages — from high-energy performance numbers such as 'Crown,' 'EL DORADO,' and 'Back It Up,' to sets with varied atmospheres including 'Crazy,' 'PLAYBOY,' 'Walking in the Memory' (기억을 걷는 밤), and 'Into Your World' (너의 세상으로).

International media outlets who attended the concerts offered glowing reviews. Forbes (US) praised the group, saying 'EXO are artists who have nothing left to prove, and every moment of dancing alongside them is a celebration in itself.' Vietnam's Tiền Phong wrote that the shows 'dispelled any concerns about the appeal of a veteran group and proved EXO's enduring vitality.' The Philippines' ABS-CBN called it 'a moment that showed once again what makes EXO, EXO.'

Meanwhile, EXO released their eighth full-length album 'REVERXE' in January. 'REVERXE' surpassed cumulative sales of one million copies, marking the group's eighth million-seller. The album's title track 'Crown' achieved five music show wins, including sweeps on all three major terrestrial broadcast music programs — Music Bank, Show! Music Core, and Inkigayo.
