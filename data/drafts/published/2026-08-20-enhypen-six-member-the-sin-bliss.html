ENHYPEN is releasing their eighth mini album, 'THE SIN : BLISS,' on August 21, 2026 — their first release as a six-member group. At a press conference held in Yongsan-gu, Seoul on the afternoon of August 20, the day before the album's release, the group stated, 'We always have the same mindset,' adding, 'From the recording stage, we focused not on the number of members but solely on how to create the best possible result.'

Jungwon shared, 'There was an Australia concert just 2 to 3 days after the team was reorganized into six members. Since we are ultimately judged by what we show on stage, we only thought about performing as professionally as possible and delivering a great show for the fans.'

The group also said, 'Starting with Seoul in May, we toured North and South America and Busan, and received a lot of energy from our ENGENE (fandom name) fans. That energy is what allowed us to work hard and prepare for this comeback.' Jake added, 'This is the first album the six of us are releasing together, and we are truly confident in it. We've come back with an exciting album, so please look forward to what's ahead for ENHYPEN.'

'THE SIN : BLISS' is the second installment in the 'THE SIN' series, which uses the concept of sin as its motif. The album depicts the conclusion of a journey faced by lovers who broke taboos and fled together, told across 10 tracks that build a detailed narrative in which the couple comes to realize that simply being together is a blessing (Bliss). Jay described it as 'a concept album where the story flows organically,' saying listeners can experience immersion like tuning into a radio show set in vampire society by listening from the narration at the start through to the tracks in order. Jungwon added that 'having a rich story to immerse yourself in is our strength.'

The album features narration by actor Park Jung-min, Japan's Tsuda Kenjiro, and China's Huang Zihongfan. The interlude track 'Breaking News,' presented in the style of a news broadcast reporting on the vampire couple's escape, features MBC 'News Desk' announcer Kim Suji, who wrote and delivered the narration herself. The album closes with 'Highlight,' an old-school hip-hop track depicting the end of a brilliant escape, and 'Hidden Truth,' which deals with the story of two people who have become a taboo to the world. Seonghun explained, 'Listening to the album, you can feel a romance where the couple's love deepens amid crisis.'

The title track 'Bloody Paradise' is a Brazilian funk song — a genre of popular music originating in Brazil characterized by heavy beats and irregular rhythms — that portrays the crisis of lovers caught in a chase and escape as a 'paradise.' It features rough distorted sounds, the textured tone of an electric guitar, and a catchy chorus melody. Julia Lewis, who produced Bad Bunny's 'DtMF,' participated in the production, and Gaeko of Dynamic Duo wrote the lyrics. Seonghun noted, 'When we first heard the title track, we felt it was different from anything we had done before,' and compared the group's music to 'tteokbokki eaten at a restaurant' — familiar yet special.

This month, alongside ENHYPEN, several major acts are making comebacks, including BIGBANG marking their 20th anniversary, Stray Kids who have topped the Billboard 200 nine times, and NCT 127 celebrating their 10th anniversary. Jungwon responded to a related question by saying, 'We are also players and fans in K-pop, so it is an honor to be active in the same month as such senior artists. We will work hard.' When asked about contract renewals — ENHYPEN will complete their full seven-year exclusive contract period next year, having debuted in 2020 after forming through the survival audition program 'I-Land' — the group replied, 'Specific discussions have not yet taken place,' and added, 'We will do our best so that there can be good news for our fans.' ENHYPEN is currently meeting fans around the world through their largest-ever world tour 'BLOOD SAGA,' with upcoming concerts in Macau in October and Tokyo and Aichi, Japan in December.
