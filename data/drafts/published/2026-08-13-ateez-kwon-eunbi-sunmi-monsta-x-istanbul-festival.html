Kwon Eunbi, ATEEZ, Sunmi, and MONSTA X are set to take the stage at Turkey's largest music festival. The Istanbul Festival, held from August 1 to 16 at Festival Park Yenikapı in Istanbul, Turkey, is organized by Focus Istanbul Etkinlik Yönetimi and is the biggest music festival in the country. Now in its fifth year, the festival has previously featured global pop stars including Jennifer Lopez.

The K-pop acts will perform at the Korea Festival segment on August 14 and 15, meeting approximately 50,000 local fans. Excitement among Turkish fans is at an all-time high following the announcement of these top-tier K-pop stars. The event was organized by the Korean Cultural Center in Turkey to commemorate Korea's Liberation Day (August 15), with the aim of reflecting on its significance and strengthening friendship between Korea and Turkey through music and culture. Jung Art & Company serves as the official Korean partner, handling planning and production of the K-pop performances.

Kwon Eunbi and ATEEZ will take the stage on August 14. Kwon Eunbi has prepared a setlist featuring hit songs including 'Door,' 'Hi,' 'Hello Stranger,' 'Unnatural,' and 'Underwater,' along with a cover of Dua Lipa's 'Levitating.'

ATEEZ will captivate the Turkish audience with their trademark intense performances, delivering a wide-ranging setlist that includes their globally popular track 'BAD,' as well as 'Adrenaline,' 'Say My Name,' 'WORK,' 'BOUNCY,' and 'WAVE.'

Sunmi, who takes the stage on Liberation Day, August 15, will perform a selection of beloved hit songs including '24 Hours,' 'Siren,' 'Forever July,' 'Violet Night,' 'Naldari,' and 'Gashina.' MONSTA X will present tracks that have earned steady love from global fans, including 'DRAMARAMA,' 'Rush Hour,' 'The Dreaming,' 'Love Killa,' 'Burning Up,' and 'ZONE.'

Lee Yeon-jeong, CEO of Jung Art & Company, which is handling the planning and production of the performances, stated: 'It is deeply meaningful to be able to commemorate Korea's Liberation Day at the Istanbul Festival — a major global event where world-class artists have performed — and to share Korean culture with local audiences through K-pop.'
