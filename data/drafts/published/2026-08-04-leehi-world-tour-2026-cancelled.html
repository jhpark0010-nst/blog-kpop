Singer LEEHI has cancelled her entire world tour schedule just four days before the first show was set to begin. According to Mydaily's reporting on August 4, LEEHI made the decision to cancel all dates of the 'LEEHI & 808 HI RECORDINGS WORLD TOUR 2026,' which had been planned across August and September.

According to insiders, there are also reported issues surrounding the reason for the cancellation. LEEHI's side claims they were unilaterally notified of the show cancellations and audience refunds, and as a result, LEEHI's team has indicated they will pursue legal proceedings to determine who is at fault. The organizer has remained silent so far.

LEEHI had planned a tour spanning approximately one month across a total of 10 cities, beginning in Singapore on August 8, followed by Jakarta (Indonesia), Manila (Philippines), Hong Kong, Bangkok (Thailand), Taiwan, Sydney, Brisbane, Melbourne (Australia), and Auckland (New Zealand). Fan anticipation had been high, as the shows were set to feature performances together with 808 HI RECORDINGS. With the conflict between the parties leading to the cancellations, attention is now focused on how responsibility will be addressed.

Earlier this year, LEEHI and Dok2 (도끼) co-founded 808 HI RECORDINGS. In the process, it was also revealed that the two have been in a relationship for five years. Dok2 posted a photo taken with LEEHI on his social media with the caption 'TO MY LADY,' and LEEHI also confirmed the relationship on her own social media, writing 'my one and only Dok2.'
