KISS OF LIFE released their third single 'SWEAT' at 6 p.m. on August 4, launching their full comeback activities. The album captures an attitude of not needing to prove one's inner effort and anxiety to others, celebrating the natural beauty and unique presence that blossoms from time built on one's own terms.

Having cemented their status as 'Summer Queens' with their previous release 'Sticky,' the group aims to present an even richer, more mature narrative with their new track 'SWEAT.' Ahead of the comeback, the members were full of excitement and confidence. Belle said, 'We're back with the title track 'SWEAT' and the b-side 'WHAT!' — two songs. I'm just happy that we get to properly show our 'Summer Queen' side this summer.' She added, 'I want to take responsibility for everyone's summer playlist.' Haneul also recalled the moment she gave a surprise preview of the chorus at a recent Waterbomb stage, saying, 'Even though we only showed a little, everyone loved it so much — it gave me a great feeling of anticipation going into this.'

Haneul explained how the new single differs in mood from 'Sticky': ''Sticky' felt like dancing on the beach with the wind blowing, while 'SWEAT' feels like dancing against a desert backdrop under blazing sunlight as the sun sets.' The title track is an Afro house-based song combining electronic pop and Latin rhythms, capturing the tension of a moment when eyes meet and a rhythmic energy. Beyond the surface meaning of sweat, the song expresses the traces of effort the members have refined behind the scenes, delivered through KISS OF LIFE's signature confident attitude.

Belle spoke about how this message connects deeply to the team's identity: 'Our team makes music with the intention of instilling confidence, freedom, and courage in our listeners, so I felt this song aligned with that. The key phrase of the song, which comes out in the chorus, is 'Don't let others see your effort and sweat.' Because KISS OF LIFE is a group that puts in a lot of effort behind the scenes and sweats a lot where no one can see, we wanted to convey the meaning of that effort and the fact that the public recognizes it.' Belle also shared the group's musical direction: 'We want to show women who can inspire confidence and self-assurance, and within the broad framework of R&B, hip-hop, and pop, we want to bring new challenges and surprises.' She added, 'We try to musically express the perfection within everyone's imperfections, and the charm that comes from things that are a little rough around the edges.'

Julie emphasized that the members are honest with each other when doubt or anxiety arises: 'When we feel anxious or uncertain, we openly share it with each other, acknowledge it, and give one another reassurance. Rather than just being endlessly confident, we want to honestly show the process we go through and deliver an empowering message to fans — that the process you feel is also something bold and wonderful.' On the performance side, Natti said, 'This time, rather than the word 'daring,' I wanted to show that it's genuinely fun to watch. I hope people enjoy themselves through this stage — it feels like a dance you'd want to follow along to and even practice at home.' She added, 'There's definitely a point choreography this time. It's a hip-swaying move that feels a little coy but very relaxed, like gently swaying on a beach.' Julie, responding to past attention around the group's bold choreography, said, 'It's not always our intention to go in that direction, but this time I'm confident because we focused on freely interpreting the stage and enjoying it on our own terms.'

Julie further stressed the deeper message behind the new song: 'I wanted to show a more mature narrative rather than just a sultry, hot image in terms of genre or performance. That's why I thought 'SWEAT' suits who we are right now and this summer.' She continued, 'We've always delivered the message 'you're perfect even if you're not perfect,' and this summer I wanted to say more than just 'be confident in yourself.' I wanted to share an empowering message and positive energy — that accepting your own sweat, effort, and moments of anxiety without letting others see them is itself something wonderful.' When asked whether 'SWEAT' could surpass the massive success of 'Sticky,' Belle said confidently, 'I think we can definitely surpass 'Sticky.' We're going into all our activities with that determination, and I had the courage to believe that if we promote hard and show our sincerity, it will reach people — including getting into the upper ranks of the Melon chart.'

KISS OF LIFE have now reached their third debut anniversary, having kept up a relentless pace of comebacks averaging every three to four months. Belle reflected, 'It's hard to believe it's already been three years — time has gone by so fast. I think it's because of the timeline of non-stop comebacks every three to four months on average. So it doesn't feel like three years in terms of how it felt, but I'm certain that the effort we've put in, the career we've built, and the bond between us as a team have grown incredibly strong.' Haneul added, 'Of course there were times when we had to prepare quickly, but once the album takes shape and you can see the results, the process itself is so enjoyable. Thinking about showing fans an even better side of ourselves with the members makes me want to get on stage faster.' Julie said, 'When we mentioned our third anniversary, a lot of people said 'Has it really only been three years?' Hearing that made me really proud — it felt like we had packed those three years completely full.' She also shared, 'The members still have far more passion for achieving things than desire for rest. Sometimes it's physically tiring and exhausting, but because our goals are clear, we motivate ourselves and are having fun and working passionately.' On future goals, Julie said, 'It would be amazing to become artists who reach number one in every category. It might sound like a clichéd answer, but we know that getting a number one isn't something that comes naturally to us, and we've felt how precious that opportunity is — so we want to keep producing results we can prove and show to our fans (Kissies).' Julie also hinted at a full-length album: 'If KISS OF LIFE releases a full album, I think it will be truly incredible. It seems like a lot of people are anticipating it, so we're always thinking about it and working hard to make sure it will be something truly amazing.' She closed with a message to fans: 'Kissies are always such a grateful presence, quietly cheering us on. We've come back with a song we think you'll really love, so I hope we can enjoy this promotions period together in good health and happiness.'
