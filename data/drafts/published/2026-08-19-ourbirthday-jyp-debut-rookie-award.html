Rookie girl group OURBIRTHDAY held a showcase at Blue Square in Yongsan-gu, Seoul on August 19, 2026, to celebrate the release of their first single, 'Our Birthday.' Despite saying they were 'so nervous,' the seven members were visibly full of excitement about their debut.

Leader Jo Hyejin expressed her feelings, saying, 'This is the debut moment we have been waiting for together with the members, so I am excited and looking forward to it.' She added, 'We gritted our teeth and worked hard to prepare for this debut. We will work hard with our own performances and music.'

OURBIRTHDAY is the first girl group from INIT Entertainment, a subsidiary of JYP Entertainment, and the first JYP girl group to debut in four years since NMIXX. The group's name carries the meaning of enjoying each moment as if every day were a birthday, redefining ordinary daily life in their own unique color.

The group is a seven-member act consisting of Jo Hyejin, winner of KBS 2TV's 'The Ddanddara,' Shin Hyewon from JTBC's 'R U Next?,' Guk Chorok, Yu, Baby, Archiraya, and Kirara. Jo Hyejin, Baby, and Archiraya were revealed ahead of the official debut through a pre-debut process, with the full group now active together.

Archiraya said, 'There is pressure being a girl group coming out of JYP after four years,' but added, 'I will use that pressure as a driving force to work even harder and show great performances and our own charm.' Yu shared that producer Park Jinyoung advised them to get along well with each other, and that the members respect and care for one another even when opinions differ, so they can promote together for a long time.

The title track of the debut single, 'SQUEEZY,' released at 6 p.m. that day, is a funk-based dance track with an addictive melody, carrying the message of shaking off complicated emotions and stress. The debut single includes a total of four tracks: 'SQUEEZY,' 'Our Birthday,' 'HUNGRY,' and one more. Guk Chorok described 'SQUEEZY' as 'a song that expresses the freedom and energy of the seven of us,' adding that she feels their confident attitude and intense performance are well captured in it. When asked about desired labels and role models, the group answered 'none,' with Guk Chorok explaining, 'Our goal is to show OURBIRTHDAY's own individuality and performance — we want to be remembered as OURBIRTHDAY itself, not by any other label.' Shin Hyewon added that she has many artists she respects, making it hard to name a specific role model. The group stated firmly that their team goal is to 'work hard and win a rookie award.'
