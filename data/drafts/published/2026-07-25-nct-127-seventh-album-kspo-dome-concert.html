NCT 127 will make their comeback with their 7th full-length album on August 24, SM Entertainment announced on July 25. The group is celebrating their 10th debut anniversary this year, and all seven members — Johnny, Taeyong, Yuta, Doyoung, Jaehyun, Jungwoo, and Haechan — recently renewed their contracts with SM Entertainment.

Specific details about the new album will be revealed at a later date.

NCT 127 will open their fifth tour, 'NEO CITY - THE REDLINE,' at the KSPO Dome (formerly known as the Gymnastics Arena) in Seoul Olympic Park from September 18 to 20. This marks their first solo concert in South Korea in one year and eight months. All three shows have sold out.

SM Entertainment described the concept: 'NCT 127 will deliver energy and intense performances through a stage that blends music, performance, and spectacular production, centered on the concept of THE REDLINE — a moment that means surpassing all limits.'

Following the Seoul concerts, NCT 127 will continue the tour in Jakarta (Indonesia), Hong Kong, Singapore, Bangkok (Thailand), and Taipei (Taiwan).
