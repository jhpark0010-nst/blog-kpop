Singer Jay Park will hold an encore concert titled '2026 Jay Park World Tour Serenades and Body Rolls in Seoul: The Replay' at Jamsil Indoor Stadium in Songpa-gu, Seoul on July 29 and 30, according to his agency More Vision on July 28.

This performance marks Jay Park's first solo concert in South Korea in approximately one year and three months, following his Seoul concert held in May of last year. At this concert, Jay Park is set to present a newly arranged stage and band setup, different from the previous concert.

After the Seoul encore concert, Jay Park will continue with tours in South America, North America, and Europe starting in September.

BTOB member Eunkwang will release a new single titled 'OUR YOUTH' on August 6, according to BTOB Company. This will be his first new release in eight months since his first full-length album 'UNFOLD' in December of last year. His agency described the new track as a youth anthem that will freshly color the sweltering summer days.

Detailed information about the new single will be revealed at a later date. Eunkwang is currently active not only as a solo artist but also as part of BTOB, and as a regular MC on the tvN variety show 'Amazing Thursday.'
