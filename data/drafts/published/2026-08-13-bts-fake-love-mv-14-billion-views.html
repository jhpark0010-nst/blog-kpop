BTS's hit song 'FAKE LOVE' has surpassed 1.4 billion views on YouTube, their agency Big Hit Music announced on August 13. The music video crossed the 1.4 billion mark at approximately 3:46 PM KST that day.

This makes 'FAKE LOVE' the sixth BTS music video to reach this view count, following 'Dynamite,' 'Boy With Luv,' 'DNA,' 'MIC Drop,' and 'IDOL.'

'FAKE LOVE' was released in May 2018 and belongs to the emo hip-hop genre, blending grunge rock guitar sounds with rhythmic trap beats. The song captures the feeling of realizing that a love once thought to be destiny was actually a lie, expressed through a distinctly somber emotional tone.

The album that includes 'FAKE LOVE,' LOVE YOURSELF 轉 Tear, became the first K-pop album ever to reach number one on the Billboard 200, the main album chart in the United States.

At the time of its release, 'FAKE LOVE' peaked at number 10 on the Billboard Hot 100 and number 42 on the UK Official Singles Chart Top 100.

BTS is currently carrying out the North American leg of their new world tour, ARIRANG.
