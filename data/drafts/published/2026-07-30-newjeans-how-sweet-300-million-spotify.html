NewJeans has added yet another 300-million-stream record on Spotify. According to Spotify data released on July 30, the double single 'How Sweet' and its title track of the same name surpassed 302,620,152 cumulative streams as of July 25. With this achievement, NewJeans now holds a total of 9 songs that have each crossed the 300 million stream mark.

'How Sweet' was released on May 24, 2024, and is a bouncy hip-hop track rooted in Miami Bass.

Meanwhile, the combined cumulative Spotify stream count for all songs NewJeans has released to date is approaching 8 billion plays. 'Ditto' and 'OMG' each have over 900 million streams; 'Super Shy' has over 800 million; 'Hype Boy' has over 700 million; 'New Jeans' and 'Attention' have each surpassed 500 million; 'ETA' has over 400 million; 'Cookie' and 'How Sweet' have each crossed 300 million; 'Hurt', 'Cool With You', and 'Supernatural' have each surpassed 200 million; and 'ASAP', 'Get Up', 'Bubble Gum', and 'Right Now' have each been played over 100 million times — demonstrating the group's overwhelming presence in the global music market.
