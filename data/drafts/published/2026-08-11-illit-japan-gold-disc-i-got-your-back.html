ILLIT has proven their standout album sales performance in the Japanese market. According to an announcement by the Recording Industry Association of Japan (RIAJ) on the 10th, ILLIT's second Japanese single 'I Got Your Back' — released by members Yunah, Minju, Moka, Wonhee, and Iroha — surpassed a cumulative shipment of 100,000 copies as of July, earning the Gold Disc 'Gold' certification. This milestone was achieved just three days after the single's release on July 29.

The RIAJ tallies cumulative shipment figures for albums each month and grants official certifications by tier: 'Gold' for 100,000 copies or more, 'Platinum' for 250,000 or more, and 'Double Platinum' for 500,000 or more. ILLIT achieved 'Gold' certification for two consecutive Japanese releases, following their first Japanese single 'Toki Yo Tomare,' which was released in September of last year.

The single 'I Got Your Back' also demonstrated strong staying power in its second week, reclaiming the No. 1 spot on Japan's Oricon Daily Singles Ranking dated August 8. This was thanks to word spreading about the title track's performances, which were showcased on major local music programs and stages.
