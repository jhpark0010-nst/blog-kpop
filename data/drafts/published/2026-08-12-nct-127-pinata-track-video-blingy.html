NCT 127 has kicked off their comeback warm-up with 'Piñata,' a b-side track from their upcoming 7th full album. On August 11 at 6 p.m. KST, the group unveiled the track video for 'Piñata' via their official YouTube channel, ahead of the album 'BLINGY' set to release on August 24.

The video unfolds a story of the members living as 'fixers' — problem-solvers navigating the heart of a fierce, bustling Seoul. Along the way, they face misunderstandings and fractures that cause them to wander and clash, but ultimately come to understand one another and emerge as a stronger, more unified whole.

'Piñata' is built on a foundation of rock and hip-hop, blending electric guitar sounds, bass, and breakbeat drums. The old-school-inspired energetic performance features choreography that highlights each member's individual charm, along with a powerful group chant moment in the chorus.

The 7th full album 'BLINGY' features five members — Johnny, Yuta, Taeyong, Jaehyun, and Haechan — with Doyoung and Jungwoo currently away on mandatory military service. The album includes a total of 9 tracks, among them the title track of the same name and 'Piñata,' all showcasing NCT 127's unique musical identity.
