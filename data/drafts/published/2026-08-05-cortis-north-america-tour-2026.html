CORTIS launched the North America leg of their first-ever tour, 'Put Your Phone Down,' starting in Toronto, Canada on August 4 (local time), according to Big Hit Music on August 5. It marks the group's first North America tour since their debut.

The North America run continues through August 16, with performances scheduled in New York, Atlanta, Irving, Los Angeles, and San Francisco.

The full tour — spanning Korea, Japan, and North America across 9 cities and 14 shows — sold out all dates well in advance. To celebrate their first debut anniversary, CORTIS will also hold commemorative concerts on August 22 and 23 at Korea University's Hwajung Gymnasium in Seoul.

In other news, rookie girl group OurBirthday will make their debut on August 19 with their first single 'Our Birthday,' according to INIT Entertainment. OurBirthday is a girl group formed by INIT Entertainment, a subsidiary of JYP Entertainment, and consists of seven members: Hyejin, Baby, Achiraya, Gukcholog, Hyewon Shin, Yu, and Kirara. The group's name reflects their ambition to freely enjoy every moment as if it were a birthday and to fill ordinary daily life with their own unique color.

Meanwhile, group KIRAS wrapped up their showcase held in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia on August 3 and 4, according to Lin Branding. They performed on stage in front of music industry professionals, press, and fans at the local 'Fast Track Entertainment Launchpad' media event. The event was organized as part of a global artist development project jointly pursued by Singapore-based entertainment company Fast Track Entertainment and Sony Music Malaysia.
