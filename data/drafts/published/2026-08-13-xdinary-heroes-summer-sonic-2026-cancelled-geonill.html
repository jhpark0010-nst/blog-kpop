Xdinary Heroes will not appear at the Japanese music festival Summer Sonic 2026, following the departure of leader and drummer Geonill amid a personal controversy. The Summer Sonic 2026 organizers announced the cancellation of the band's appearance via their official social media on August 13, just one day before the scheduled Tokyo performance.

The organizers stated, 'Xdinary Heroes' appearance has been cancelled following a withdrawal request submitted by their agency,' and added, 'We sincerely apologize to all fans who were looking forward to seeing them perform.' They also confirmed that 'there will be no replacement artist for Xdinary Heroes' cancelled slot.'

Xdinary Heroes had originally been scheduled to perform at the Pacific Stage and Massive Stage of Summer Sonic 2026 on August 14 and August 16, at Makuhari Messe in Tokyo and Maishima Sports Island in Osaka, respectively. The cancellation notice was issued immediately after Geonill's departure from the group was announced the same day.

In recent days, posts and phone call recordings allegedly made by a person claiming to be Geonill's former partner had spread across social media and online communities. The individual claimed that the male voice in the recordings was Geonill's and alleged that he had made insulting and derogatory remarks directed at fans. However, it has not been confirmed whether the male voice in the released recordings is actually Geonill's.

Amid the ongoing controversy, JYP Entertainment officially announced Geonill's departure from the group and the termination of his exclusive contract on August 13. The agency stated, 'We have been deeply aware of the gravity of the situation regarding member Geonill and have had thorough discussions with the artist from multiple angles.' It added, 'As a result, we determined that it would be difficult to continue team activities together, and have agreed to terminate the exclusive contract by mutual agreement.'

The agency further stated, 'Accordingly, Geonill has left Xdinary Heroes and concluded his team activities effective today.' With the sudden departure of their leader and the cancellation of their scheduled overseas festival appearances, Xdinary Heroes now face significant changes ahead. The group is expected to continue activities as a five-member unit going forward.
