KATSEYE, the Korea-US joint girl group from HYBE, has broken into the upper ranks of Spotify's charts with their new song 'Hootie Frutti.' According to the latest Spotify charts dated August 16, 'Hootie Frutti' — the title track of KATSEYE's third mini album WILD — entered the Daily Top Songs USA at No. 6.

The track also climbed to No. 12 on the Daily Top Songs Global chart. Among all songs that newly entered both charts on that day, 'Hootie Frutti' achieved the highest ranking.

'Hootie Frutti' is a pop track built around percussion, heavy drum beats, and witty lyrics. The music video, which highlights the members' confident expressions and dynamic performances, surpassed 10 million views on YouTube within 36 hours of its release.

Beyond 'Hootie Frutti,' every track from the WILD album entered the Daily Top Songs Global chart. The pre-release single 'Animal' ranked at No. 5, followed by 'PINKY UP' at No. 84, 'Bel Air' at No. 116, and 'That Way' at No. 140.

KATSEYE also placed their previously released song 'Gabriela' at No. 152, and 'ICONIC BY MISTAKE' — a collaboration with LE SSERAFIM and ILLIT — at No. 86 on the Daily Top Songs Global chart. With a total of 7 songs on the chart simultaneously, KATSEYE became the artist with the most entries on that chart.
