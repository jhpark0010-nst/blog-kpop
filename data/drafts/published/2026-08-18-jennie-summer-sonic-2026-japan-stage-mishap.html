BLACKPINK's Jennie has shared her heartfelt feelings after experiencing a series of unexpected incidents during her solo performance at Summer Sonic 2026 in Osaka, Japan on August 16. The show, held at Expo '70 Commemorative Park, was part of her roughly three-month festival run as a solo artist.

On August 17, Jennie posted a message on her social media expressing gratitude to fans. 'I want to thank you for the love and energy you've shared with me at every festival over the past three months,' she wrote. She also added, 'It's hard to put into words what I've experienced, learned, and grown through.'

During the Osaka performance, both microphone and wardrobe issues arose one after another. Jennie was seen checking her equipment mid-show and alerting staff to the situation. A wardrobe problem also occurred, requiring her to pause and adjust her outfit on stage.

As a result of the back-to-back issues, Jennie was unable to perform two songs from her planned setlist and briefly stepped off the stage. Footage from the venue captured her biting her lip as she composed herself, and trembling hands as she brushed her hair back.

However, Jennie returned to the stage after resolving the equipment and wardrobe issues and continued her performance, completing the rest of her set for the audience.
