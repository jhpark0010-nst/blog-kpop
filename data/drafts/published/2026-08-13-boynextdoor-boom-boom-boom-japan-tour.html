BOYNEXTDOOR is set to make a major push in Japan with a new single and a multi-city tour. The six-member boy group will release their second Japanese digital single, 'Boom Boom Boom,' on August 18 at midnight.

Before the single drops, BOYNEXTDOOR will make their first live performance of the new track on TBS program 'CDTV Live! Live!' airing on August 17 at 7 p.m.

Three days after the single's release, on August 21, BOYNEXTDOOR will kick off the Japan leg of their first world tour, titled BOYNEXTDOOR TOUR 'KNOCK ON Vol.2' IN JAPAN. The tour spans six cities, starting in Kanagawa and continuing through Saga, Osaka, Miyagi, Nagano, and Chiba. The Osaka and Chiba shows have already sold out.

BOYNEXTDOOR is a six-member boy group under KOZ Entertainment, a label within HYBE. They debuted on May 30, 2023, and released their first full-length album, 'HOME,' in June.
