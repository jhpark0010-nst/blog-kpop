Singer and musical actress IVY made history as the first Korean performer to appear in a Broadway musical, taking the stage in Chicago at New York's Ambassador Theatre. She began her run on August 17 (local time) and will perform as Roxie Hart through September 6, 2026, delivering all dialogue and musical numbers in English.

On August 18, IVY shared several photos taken in New York on her social media, writing, 'Mom, I made it onto the Broadway billboard.' The photos showed a large advertisement for the musical Chicago displayed in the heart of Broadway, featuring IVY in character alongside the text 'IVY IS ROXIE HART.'

The road to Broadway was a long one. After receiving an audition invitation from the Broadway production last year, IVY completed three video auditions over the course of approximately one year before ultimately being cast as Roxie Hart.

IVY's connection to Chicago spans well over a decade. She first played Roxie Hart in the Korean production in 2012 and went on to participate in six seasons through 2024, performing the role a total of 592 times and making it her signature musical character.

Before departing for New York, IVY spoke at a press conference, saying, 'I dug at one well for a long time, and an incredible opportunity came my way.' She added, 'Thanks to the help of many people, I was given the chance to perform in the home of musical theatre.' She also shared that while she feels the weight of representing Korean musical performers, she is excited, and expressed her determination to experience firsthand how Broadway actors and staff work.

Chicago is one of Broadway's longest-running musicals, having won six Tony Awards in 1997 including Best Musical Revival, as well as the Grammy Award for Best Musical Theatre Album. After completing her Broadway run, IVY is set to return to the stage as Roxie Hart once more in December at LG Arts Center Seoul. She originally debuted as a singer in 2005, with hit songs including 'If You're Going to Be Like This' and 'Sonata of Temptation,' before expanding her career into musical theatre.
