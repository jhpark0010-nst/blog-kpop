FANOMENON (패노메논), a large-scale K-culture festival centered on K-pop, will be held for the first time from December 2 to 12, 2027, at Seoul Arena in Changdong and KINTEX Exhibition Hall 2 in Goyang. The Popular Culture Exchange Committee (대중문화교류위원회), co-chaired by Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism Choi Hwi-young and JYP Entertainment's chief producer Park Jin-young, announced the plans at a press conference held in Jongno-gu, Seoul on July 27.

Minister Choi stated the purpose of the event: 'As K-culture is receiving attention from around the world, its prosperity must continue and expand.' He added, 'Our primary goal is to earn the trust of the global entertainment industry and make this a place everyone truly wants to visit. I am excited to be announcing this, and I hope December next year comes quickly.'

FANOMENON is a privately led large-scale event combining K-pop concerts, a fandom awards ceremony, and exhibition and experience programs by K-culture companies. The event name is a portmanteau of 'fan' and 'phenomenon,' meaning 'a phenomenon created by fans,' with the intent to view fans not merely as consumers but as active partners in spreading K-culture.

Park Jin-young outlined three pillars of the initiative: 'First, the FANOMENON Awards will be held next December. Second, starting in May 2028 in Los Angeles, we will hold a Coachella-style FANOMENON Festival overseas. Third, we plan to build K-culture centers in key cities around the world — venues with around 20,000 seats that also house government offices, various companies, and stores representing Korea.' He also said, 'The starting point is viewing K-culture from the perspective of the fandom industry. If we define this fandom industry and South Korea becomes its leader, it can be a great and lasting source of strength for our country.'

The FANOMENON Awards ceremony will be held at Seoul Arena, a 20,000-seat venue scheduled for completion in May next year. The awards will honor the 10 fandom teams that showed the greatest passion and influence over the year, with artists accepting awards on behalf of their fans. Headline artist performances and exhibitions celebrating the Top 10 teams will also take place. At KINTEX during the same period, festival-style music concerts featuring multiple artists will be held alongside K-culture exhibitions and experience events, with participation from companies in food, beauty, fashion, webtoons, film, drama, animation, and more. Additional side events include fan meetings with singers and actors, production press conferences, film screenings, gaming performances, e-sports competitions, film and drama OST concerts, and K-culture seminars.

The committee plans to hold the FANOMENON Awards domestically every December and to host the FANOMENON Festival overseas starting in Los Angeles in May. Park Jin-young explained: 'Since the goal is to introduce K-culture to international audiences, it is important to gather local attendees, and for that reason it makes sense to hold the festival abroad. Considering interest, reach, and economic impact, we decided to hold the first festival in Los Angeles, the entertainment capital of the world.' The Korea Culture and Tourism Institute projected that FANOMENON would attract a total of 520,000 visitors — including 200,000 international tourists — and generate approximately 1 trillion won in economic impact. FANOMENON is being driven by a joint venture (JV) formed by Korea's four major entertainment companies: HYBE, SM, YG, and JYP. The JV is currently undergoing a pre-merger business review process.

Park Jin-young also expressed his ambition to make the FANOMENON Awards as prestigious as the Academy Awards or the Grammy Awards. 'Just imagining the world's eyes turning to Seoul every December is already amazing,' he said. He then addressed the press directly: 'When I used to say I wanted to break into the US market with K-pop, people said things like, why are you dragging the Wonder Girls over there for nothing, or that I had an America obsession — and those words really hurt. But looking at TWICE and Stray Kids now, I think what Bang Si-hyuk and I used to rack our brains over in some corner of America has become reality. That picture is so wonderful that, even though this is an outrageous and overwhelming undertaking, I want to try. Please give us your support.'
