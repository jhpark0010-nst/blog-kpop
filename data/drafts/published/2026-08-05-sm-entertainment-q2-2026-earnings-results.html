SM Entertainment (KOSDAQ: 041510) announced on August 5 that its consolidated operating profit for Q2 2026 came in at 52.9 billion KRW, a 11% increase compared to the same period last year. Revenue reached 349.6 billion KRW, up 15.4% year-on-year. Net profit, however, fell 5.6% to 29.2 billion KRW.

The Q2 operating profit figure matched the market consensus of 52.9 billion KRW as compiled by Yonhap Infomax.

SM attributed the results to concert revenue growth driven by expanded performance scale, as well as broader revenue improvements from key subsidiaries. The company also noted that concert tour expansions by senior artists, along with advertising, events, and other IP-based revenue from its artists, are continuing to diversify its overall revenue structure.

On a standalone basis, SM's Q2 revenue was 240.6 billion KRW, up 9.2% year-on-year. During the same period, concert revenue grew 23.6%, appearance revenue rose 27.9%, and MD and licensing revenue increased 22.0%.

SM stated it plans to maintain active activity in the global music market throughout Q3 and Q4 with a diverse artist lineup. In Q3, releases include a Red Velvet mini album, an NCT 127 full album, mini albums from Minho and WayV, and a single from Girls' Generation unit Hyoryusu. In Q4, full albums from Yesung, Taeyeon, Jaehyun, and NCT DREAM are scheduled, along with mini albums from Choikang Changmin, Xiaojun, and NCT WISH, and a single from Hearts2Hearts.

In the second half of the year, SM also has aespa's new world tour planned, along with concert tours from U-Know Yunho, Super Junior-83Z, Yesung, Ryeowook, NCT 127, and WayV, as well as a fan meeting tour by NCT WISH.
