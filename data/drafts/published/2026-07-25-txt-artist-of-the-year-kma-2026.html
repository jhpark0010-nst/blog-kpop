TOMORROW X TOGETHER (TXT) took home the top honor at KMA 2026 (KM Chart Awards 2026), winning the Artist of the Year award. The ceremony was held on July 26 at Hwajung Gymnasium, Korea University, in Seongbuk-gu, Seoul.

Actor Byun Woo-seok appeared as the presenter for the top award at the ceremony. He announced TOMORROW X TOGETHER as the Artist of the Year recipients. After accepting the award, the members expressed their emotion, saying, 'We will come back soon with an even better album.'

Also at the ceremony, CORTIS won the Record of the Year award. The CORTIS members shared, ''Red Red' was a song we had confidence in from the very first moment we started making it.' They added, 'Hwajung Gymnasium is the very place where we held our first concert, so we are even more overjoyed to receive this meaningful award here.'

KMA 2026 is hosted by KM Chart and KM Chart World, and organized by the KMA Organizing Committee and UPICK. The awards ceremony is a music festival and awards show dedicated to the artists who have lit up the Korean music scene over the past year and the fans who have supported them.

The performers and attendees at the ceremony included TOMORROW X TOGETHER, P1Harmony, Hi-Fi Un!corn, ZEROBASEONE, 82MAJOR, UNIS, VVUP, KickFlip, Hearts2Hearts, NEWBEAT, ALLDAY PROJECT, CORTIS, ALPHA DRIVE ONE, LNGSHOT, and MODYSSEY (listed in order of debut).
