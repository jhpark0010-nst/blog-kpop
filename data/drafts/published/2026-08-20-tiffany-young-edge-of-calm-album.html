Girls' Generation's Tiffany Young released her first solo full-length album, Edge Of Calm, at 6 p.m. on August 20, according to her agency Pacific Music Group. The album marks a significant milestone as her debut solo studio record.

Edge Of Calm is an album that captures the subtle tension, focus, and crossing of emotional balance in the moments just before stillness. Drawing on a deepened musical sensibility, Tiffany Young unfolds a delicate emotional thread throughout the record.

The album includes 11 tracks: double title songs 'Edge Of Calm' and 'Lips Together,' along with 'Codes,' 'Drip Drop,' 'Halfway Honest,' 'Moment,' 'Lovers,' and more.

The title track 'Edge Of Calm' captures the moment of quietly embracing someone caught in waves of anxiety and emotion, staying by their side without words. Tiffany Young sings of a love that is like a 'safe space' — where two people can briefly lean on each other at the boundary where chaos passes through.

'Lips Together' features (G)I-DLE's Miyeon. The song expresses the feeling of wanting a wounded heart to be healed through love.

Tiffany Young participated in the album's concept planning, styling, visual directing, and music video story development. She also recently appeared as Kim Yumi in the original musical 'Yumi's Cells.'
