BTS member V revealed on August 12 that he has been receiving treatment for a hearing problem. He made the confession during a Weverse Live session he conducted together with fellow member Jungkook.

During the live broadcast, V said, 'I have never told the ARMY (fandom name) about this, but my ears have not been well for the past two and a half years.' He added, 'If my left ear hears at around 100, my right ear only hears at 30. So I have been consistently taking medication. I also make sure to go to the hospital regularly.'

The two members shared an honest conversation over a meal together after completing their concert in Baltimore, USA, on the 11th (local time).

Jungkook also opened up about his own physical condition, saying, 'My shin is in a state just before a stress fracture. Even today on stage, my heart wanted to run around like crazy, but it hurt so much in between that I couldn't. I hope (the fans) will understand.'

He further explained, 'I will perform while maintaining (my condition) as best as I can. It has not reached a stress fracture yet, but there is a lot of inflammation in my shin. It seems there is also some minor damage to the bone.' Jungkook added, 'I definitely need to run when I have to run, but I need to pace myself in between — even if I have to force it — in order to get through this tour well.'

BTS is currently on the North American leg of their new world tour 'ARIRANG.' They are set to meet fans at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, USA, on August 15 and 16, and at Rogers Stadium in Toronto, Canada, on August 22 and 23.
