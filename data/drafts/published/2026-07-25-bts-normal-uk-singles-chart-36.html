BTS track 'NORMAL' has re-entered the UK Official Singles Chart Top 100, debuting at No. 36 as of July 24 (local time). The song had previously charted at No. 74 when it was first released in March.

'NORMAL' is an alternative pop track that calmly portrays the emptiness and fear that exist behind the spotlight. The song features world-renowned producer Ryan Tedder and is characterized by a smooth, conversational singing-rap style.

The track successfully re-entered the chart on the strength of its music video release and the unveiling of a Korean-language version on July 17.

'NORMAL' is included on BTS's fifth album 'ARIRANG', which landed at No. 41 on the UK Official Albums Chart Top 100, marking its 18th consecutive week on the chart.

Meanwhile, 'GOLDEN', the original soundtrack from the Netflix animated film 'K-Pop Demon Hunters', climbed two spots from the previous week to No. 47 on the UK Official Singles Chart Top 100, marking its 57th consecutive week on the chart.
