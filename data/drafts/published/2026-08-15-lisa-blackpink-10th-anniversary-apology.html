Lisa of BLACKPINK has issued a belated apology regarding the controversy surrounding the group's 10th debut anniversary event, becoming the third member to personally express regret to fans, following Jisoo and Rosé.

On August 14, Lisa wrote on a fan communication platform: 'Our 10th anniversary was already almost a week ago. As everyone knows, there has been a lot of talk online.' She then added, 'I am so sad about the situation, and I am truly sorry for the way things unfolded. I cannot even imagine how upset you must have felt — because I felt the same way.'

Lisa continued, 'It should have been a special moment and a time of celebration for all of us. I am not writing this to justify anything or to make a point. I simply want to cherish the love and bond we have shared.' She also said, 'I hope we can continue to move forward together, holding onto the love we have shared. I love BLINKs, and thank you for always being by our side.'

BLACKPINK had held a 10th anniversary meet-and-greet event on August 8 — their debut date — at the National Museum of Korea in Yongsan-gu, Seoul. All four members, Jennie, Jisoo, Rosé, and Lisa, attended, but fan complaints arose over the planning and communication process. The event was announced only two days before it took place, with an entry application window of just approximately nine hours. Only 40 paid membership holders, selected by lottery, were allowed to attend in person.

The initial announcement stated that members would attend 'according to their schedules,' causing confusion over whether all four would be present. Full attendance was confirmed only the day before the event, and the specific venue was revealed on the day itself. Fans also noted that the scale of the 10th anniversary event was smaller than the 8th anniversary event, at which 88 fans had been invited.

As the controversy spread, Jisoo apologized during a live broadcast on the day of the event, saying, 'I know the 10th anniversary is an important day. It seems there were issues in the communication process.' She was later spotted in tears inside a vehicle, but clarified, 'I hope people do not read too many meanings into my tears,' explaining that they reflected her emotion over the ten years shared together. Rosé also subsequently apologized, saying, 'I had been looking forward to the 10th anniversary just as much as BLINKs, so I felt a great sense of disappointment. I feel heavy-hearted thinking I may have made you feel lonelier during that time. I wanted it to be a time when everyone could smile and be happy, and I am sorry it did not work out that way.'

On the day of the event, Lisa had posted on her own social media: 'Thank you so much to all the BLINKs who have been with us for the past 10 years,' sharing her thoughts on the anniversary, but did not directly address the controversy over how the event was run at that time. It appears that after fans' feelings of disappointment continued, she published a separate post approximately one week later to express her apology. BLACKPINK debuted on August 8, 2016, and celebrated their 10th debut anniversary this year.
