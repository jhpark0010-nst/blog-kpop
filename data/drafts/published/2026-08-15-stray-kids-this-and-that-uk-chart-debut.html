Stray Kids have charted both their new mini album 'THIS & THAT' and its title track on the UK Official Singles and Albums Charts Top 100. According to the Official Charts dated August 14 (local time), the title track 'THIS & THAT' debuted at No. 61 on the Singles Chart Top 100.

This marks the eighth time a Stray Kids song has entered the UK Official Singles Chart, following 'MANIAC', 'Miroh' (특), 'Rock' (樂), 'LOSE MY BREATH', 'CHK CHK BOOM', 'CEREMONY', and 'DO IT'.

The mini album of the same name made its first appearance on the Albums Chart Top 100, debuting at No. 80. 'THIS & THAT' is an album that captures Stray Kids' bold attitude and confidence in pursuing what they want without being bound by convention. The title track reflects a chill and relaxed side of the group.

Also on the Singles Chart Top 100, KATSEYE's 'ANIMAL' charted at No. 16, and 'GOLDEN', the original soundtrack from the Netflix animated film 'K-Pop Demon Hunters', came in at No. 45. 'ICONIC BY MISTAKE', a collaboration track by LE SSERAFIM, ILLIT, and KATSEYE, entered at No. 93.

Meanwhile, BTS's fifth full-length album 'ARIRANG' continued its run on the Albums Chart Top 100, entering at No. 59 for its 21st consecutive week.
