Allegations have emerged that the Grammy Awards has deleted BTS videos from its official YouTube channel. As of July 30, the performance videos of 'Dynamite' and 'Butter' — which BTS performed at the Grammys in 2021 and 2022 respectively — are no longer visible on the Grammy channel.

Both videos had accumulated notably high view counts on the Grammy channel, but for reasons unknown they can no longer be found there. The videos remain available only on BTS's official YouTube channel.

All seven BTS members posted a statement on their individual SNS accounts on July 29, saying: 'We have decided not to submit to the Grammys this year. We hope our music can be heard and loved as music itself, rather than being categorized by region or language.' They added: 'We are grateful to ARMY (their fandom name) and everyone who has always been with us.'

BTS's fifth full-length album 'ARIRANG,' released in March, topped both the Billboard main singles chart and main albums chart in the United States. The group's decision is widely interpreted as a criticism of the Grammy's announcement last month that it would create a new 'Asian Pop Music Performance' category, separating out music from Asian countries including South Korea, China, and Japan.

On the same day, Harvey Mason Jr., CEO of the Recording Academy — the organization that oversees the Grammys — posted a statement on the Grammy's official SNS: 'This is not about separating anyone, but about expanding the range of artists recognized by the 15,000 Grammy voting members.'

Comments calling out the video deletions have been flooding the Grammy YouTube channel. One user wrote: 'You deleted butter and dynamite?? Bro note it grammy's downfall just started.'
