KATSEYE member Sophia is pausing her activities due to health reasons, HYBE and Geffen Records announced on August 8. The label shared the news officially, stating that Sophia will be unable to participate in upcoming promotional activities for the time being.

HYBE and Geffen Records stated, 'Above all, we must prioritize Sophia's mental health and stability. Sophia is receiving full support, and following in-depth consultations with medical professionals, she has been advised to take sufficient rest and commit to ongoing care for a full recovery.' The label added, 'Sophia herself deeply wishes to be on stage with the other members, but the health, safety, and long-term well-being of our artists will always remain our top priority.'

Sophia also shared a message on social media the same day. 'It truly breaks my heart not to be able to take part in so many things. You all know how much I love standing on stage, performing, and sharing these moments with you,' she wrote. 'Making this decision was never easy, but I am learning that health must come first.'

She continued, 'I am realizing that if I do not take care of my body and mind right now, I will not be able to continue doing what I love most for a long time.'

KATSEYE member Manoban had previously paused activities in February due to health issues. With Sophia now also stepping back, the group will promote as a four-member unit — Daniela, Megan, Lara, and Yoonchae — for the foreseeable future.
