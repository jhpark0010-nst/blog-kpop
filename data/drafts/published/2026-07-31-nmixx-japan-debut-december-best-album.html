NMIXX will officially debut in Japan on December 9, 2026, releasing their best album titled 'N=MIXX.' JYP Entertainment announced the news on July 31.

The best album will include 12 tracks in total, featuring NMIXX's representative song 'Blue Valentine' as well as Japanese-language versions of previously released songs.

Ahead of the Japan debut, NMIXX will hold a solo concert at Keio Arena Tokyo on August 8 and 9, where they will meet fans in person. The group also recently wrapped up a concert in Hong Kong on July 25 and 26.
