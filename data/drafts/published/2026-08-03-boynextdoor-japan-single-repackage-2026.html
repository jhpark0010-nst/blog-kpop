BOYNEXTDOOR is gearing up for a busy second half of the year, announcing a new Japan digital single release and a first full album repackage. The six-member group — Sungho, Riwoo, Myungjae, Taesan, Leehan, and Woonhak — held their tour 'BOYNEXTDOOR TOUR KNOCK ON Vol.2 IN BUSAN' at Busan Sajik Indoor Gymnasium from July 31 to August 2.

Following the Busan concerts, BOYNEXTDOOR shared their feelings: 'We are truly happy to have been able to perform in Busan after Seoul. We thought hard and prepared diligently to leave you with unforgettable memories. Thank you for being with us, and we hope to stay happy together with WONDERs going forward.'

During the performances, the group also made a special announcement: 'We will release our second digital single in Japan on August 18, and on September 28 we will meet even more fans with a first full album repackage.'

This will mark BOYNEXTDOOR's first Japanese release in approximately one year, since August of last year. The group will also hold 'BOYNEXTDOOR TOUR KNOCK ON Vol.2 IN JAPAN' at K-Arena Yokohama in Kanagawa from August 21 to 23.

Following the repackage release, the group will carry out domestic activities in October. They will wrap up their Japan tour in Chiba from October 10 to 11.

BOYNEXTDOOR is a six-member boy group under KOZ Entertainment, a label within HYBE, and debuted on May 30, 2023.
