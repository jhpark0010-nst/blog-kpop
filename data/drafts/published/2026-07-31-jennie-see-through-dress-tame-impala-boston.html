BLACKPINK's Jennie turned heads with a bold see-through look. On July 31, she posted several photos to her account along with a heart emoji, sharing backstage behind-the-scenes shots from the event.

On July 30 (local time), Jennie made a surprise appearance at Tame Impala's concert in Boston, USA, performing the first-ever live joint stage of their collaboration track 'Dracula.' The backstage photos she shared captured the full look from that night.

Jennie layered a red-patterned slip top with lace detailing under a black see-through dress featuring a daring slit, radiating a one-of-a-kind aura. She completed the styling with bold lace-patterned tights that hugged her leg line and strappy heels, perfectly pulling off a chic yet alluring lingerie-inspired look.

Jennie's relaxed and witty demeanor also caught attention throughout the photos.

Meanwhile, Jennie's collaboration track 'Dracula' with Tame Impala has charted at No. 5 on the US Billboard main singles chart Hot 100 for two consecutive weeks, marking her personal best position on the chart.
