TWICE member Jeongyeon (real name Yu Jeongyeon) has left JYP Entertainment after 11 years since her debut and signed an exclusive contract with Baro Entertainment, the acting agency where her older sister Kong Seung-yeon (real name Yu Seung-yeon) is also signed.

Baro announced the signing through an official press release, stating, 'We are sincerely delighted to have formed a precious connection with Yu Jeongyeon, who possesses diverse charms and limitless potential,' and added, 'We deeply resonated with Yu Jeongyeon's sincere attitude and genuine passion for acting.'

Yu Jeongyeon plans to take on new challenges as an actress going forward. She will continue her activities with TWICE at the same time.

Yu Jeongyeon shared her feelings via social media, saying, 'Leaving a familiar place was frightening, but I was able to find courage because of the members who believed in me and ONCE (TWICE's fandom name).' She added, 'Although I am moving to a new home and starting a new challenge, I will continue to cherish TWICE — the greatest center within me — just as I always have.'

She further emphasized, 'Standing before ONCE as a member of TWICE will always come first.'

Yu Jeongyeon debuted with girl group TWICE in 2015 and has released numerous hit songs, including 'T.T', 'Cheer Up', and 'LIKEY'.
