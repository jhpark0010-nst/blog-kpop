Cha Eun-woo is set to transform into a Michelin-starred chef. According to MyDaily reporting on August 12, Cha Eun-woo — who is scheduled to be discharged from military service in January of next year — has already confirmed his comeback project as the lead of the drama 'Seorabeol Knife.'

'Seorabeol Knife' is a limited-edition gastronomic romance between an Italian chef known as 'the temptation of the Mediterranean' and a princess destined to become the sun of Seorabeol (the ancient capital of the Silla Kingdom). The drama combines the concepts of time-slip and cooking.

Cha Eun-woo will play Kim Jae-gyeom, the owner and head chef of a Michelin 1-star restaurant. One day, while agonizing over how to reach the pinnacle of a perfect 3-star rating, he tastes wild honey left behind by a father he has never met in his life — and in that instant, he is forcibly summoned back 1,000 years in time to the Silla Kingdom. The man who once used his hands to earn European stars now lifts the lid of a Silla cooking pot, striking the royal kitchen (the Silla palace kitchen, known as jeonseonseo) like a bolt from the blue — making him Silla's greatest overnight 3-star.

Following the release of the Netflix series 'Wonderfuls' during his time in the military, Cha Eun-woo received offers from numerous projects for his comeback, and he chose 'Seorabeol Knife' among them. This marks the first time since his debut that he will take on the role of a chef.

'Seorabeol Knife' follows the head chef of the Michelin 1-star restaurant Orot, who time-slips in accordance with a 1,000-year prophecy contained within a gold-inlaid wild honey box, and must prove the true realm of 'taste' to a princess — the sole sacred-bone royalty of Silla and a fastidious gourmet who craves the finest flavors in the world. It is a 1,000-year romance between a person walking the lonely path of a queen and a chef who has crossed 1,000 years of time and space, crafting perfect flavors with their hands to be savored on the tongue.

The team behind 'The Tyrant's Chef' is joining forces on this project. Director Jang Tae-yu will helm the drama and writer Kim Ji-young will pen the script. Discussions are currently underway for the drama to air on tvN.
