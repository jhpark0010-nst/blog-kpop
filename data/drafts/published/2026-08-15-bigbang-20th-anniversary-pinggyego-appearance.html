BIGBANG is reuniting as a full group to mark their 20th debut anniversary, appearing together on the YouTube variety show 'Pinggyego' hosted by Yoo Jae-suk. On August 15, a teaser video titled '[Hint Cam] The 116th Gathering Attendee' was released on the YouTube channel 'Deun Deun,' previewing the upcoming episode.

In the teaser, Yoo Jae-suk introduced the new guests by saying, 'There are big events in August — my birthday, a 20th debut anniversary, and Haha's birthday.' The guests' faces were hidden on screen, but the '20th debut anniversary' hint strongly suggested BIGBANG's appearance.

G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung all appear together in this episode of 'Pinggyego,' marking a rare full-group outing on a variety program. The three members sit down with Yoo Jae-suk and Haha to talk about their recent activities, hit songs, and each member's individual dance style.

BIGBANG's recent Coachella performance also came up as a topic. Yoo Jae-suk said, 'I never expected to sing 'Nal Bwa Gwisun' there,' and Haha added, 'And they went ahead and did a reggae arrangement.' G-Dragon then revealed, 'When I needed a reggae arrangement, I thought maybe I should contact Haha.' Haha showed excitement at this, but Yoo Jae-suk immediately asked, 'But you didn't contact him, did you?' When G-Dragon replied, 'I didn't,' Yoo Jae-suk shot back, 'Good,' drawing laughter.

The group's extensive catalog of hit songs over 20 years also came up. Yoo Jae-suk marveled, 'How many crowd-singalong hits would there be if you counted all the solo songs too?' Haha agreed, saying, 'There really isn't a single song I don't know.' Youngest member Daesung added some humor by comparing Taeyang's dance style to 'neatly cut pumpkin taffy' and G-Dragon's to 'stretchy ginger taffy,' even acting out the moves himself. G-Dragon fired back, 'So in the end, are you saying we both dance like taffy?' A flustered Daesung clarified, 'No, I mean it's chewy and elastic,' while Taeyang playfully piled on, saying, 'What a comparison...'

Taeyang also brought back his once-viral catchphrase 'I missed you so much,' a line that had become a talking point on past variety shows. BIGBANG debuted in 2006 and has produced numerous hit songs including 'Lies,' 'Last Farewell,' 'Haru Haru,' 'FANTASTIC BABY,' and 'BANG BANG BANG,' earning widespread love at home and abroad. The full episode of 'Pinggyego' featuring BIGBANG will be released on August 22 at 9 a.m. KST on the YouTube channel 'Deun Deun.'
