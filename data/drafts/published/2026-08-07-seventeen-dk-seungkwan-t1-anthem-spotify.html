SEVENTEEN members DK and Seungkwan are set to collaborate with global esports organization T1 in a unique musical project. According to their agency Pledis Entertainment on August 7, the two will release T1's official anthem 'One - Spotify Singles' simultaneously worldwide through the global music platform Spotify on August 10 at 6 p.m.

The new track 'One - Spotify Singles' is a modern pop-punk song that blends intense electric guitar sounds with a driving, high-energy chorus. The song carries a narrative of trust and solidarity that transcends limits, and the two artists' powerful vocals bursting over a building band sound are set to deliver a refreshing rush of excitement.

Spotify Korea, which planned this project, explained the reasoning behind the selection: 'We determined that DK and Seungkwan's wide-ranging musical expressiveness and overwhelming vocal ability were the perfect fit for conveying the energy of the T1 anthem.'

A teaser video released on August 5 also drew significant attention. It takes the form of a fake documentary in which T1's players — Doran, Oner, Faker, Peyz, and Keria — transform into rookie idol group 'T1 STARS' and have their debut song stolen by their seniors DK and Seungkwan. The two's deadpan acting had fans in stitches.

DK and Seungkwan are also scheduled to perform live on stage at 'LCK Team Road Show: T1 Home Ground,' to be held at KSPO Dome in Seoul Olympic Park on August 14.
