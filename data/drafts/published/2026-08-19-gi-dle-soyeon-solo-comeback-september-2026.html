(G)I-DLE leader and producer Soyeon is returning as a solo artist after five years. She has confirmed a solo comeback for early September 2026 and has begun full preparations for her activities. This marks approximately five years and two months since her first solo mini-album 'Windy', released in July 2021.

As the group's overall producer, Soyeon has written and composed numerous mega-hit songs including 'TOMBOY', 'Nxde', 'Queencard', and 'Fate', establishing herself as an irreplaceable self-producing artist. In 2024, she was elevated to full membership in the Korea Music Copyright Association, and she was also named to Forbes' '30 Under 30 Asia' list, earning recognition for her exceptional musical influence on the global stage.

More recently, under her producer name 'icebluerabbit', she participated in the digital single 'Mono (Feat. skaiwater)', released in January. She also performed her solo track 'ICE BLUE RABBIT' during the fourth world tour '2026 i-dle WORLD TOUR [Syncopation]', drawing an enthusiastic response from fans. In addition, she has collaborated with top-tier international musicians including American rapper Odetari and Grammy-winning singer-songwriter Anderson .Paak, showcasing an ever-expanding musical spectrum.
