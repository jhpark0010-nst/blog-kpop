SHINee's Minho is set to return to the K-pop scene in September 2026. According to MyDaily's reporting on August 12, Minho will release his second mini album in early September and make his solo comeback. He has recently finished filming the music video for his new track and is in the final stages of preparation.

This upcoming album will be his first new release in approximately nine months, following his single 'TEMPO,' which came out in December 2025.

Minho officially debuted as a solo artist in 2022 with his first mini album 'CHASE.' He then released his first full-length album 'CALL BACK' in 2024, expanding his musical range and establishing his own distinct solo identity.

Last year, he held a solo fan meeting and also took the stage in theatrical productions 'Rendezvous' and 'Waiting for Waiting for Godot,' while maintaining a steady presence on variety shows and staying active across multiple fields. In June of this year, he continued his group activities as SHINee released their sixth mini album 'Atmos.'

Having remained active both as a group member and as a solo artist, Minho is now set to meet fans once again through a solo album. Attention is focused on what new side he will show through his second mini album, having already built a clear identity as a solo artist.
