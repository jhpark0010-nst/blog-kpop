BIGBANG has unveiled a group visual photo for their upcoming new single. On August 11, YG Entertainment posted '[BiiiG] VISUAL PHOTO - iii ver.' on their official social media channels.

In the visual photo, the BIGBANG members radiated an untouchable charisma within a space bathed in intense red light, leaving a powerful impression. Above all, the members commanded attention through sheer presence and gesture alone, without any elaborate props, while bold styling — including accessory-adorned jackets, studded belts, and python-pattern tops — created an extraordinary atmosphere.

As teaser content has been unveiled one after another, anticipation among global fans has reached its peak. Fans are enjoying speculation based on various clues including colors such as blue, red, and yellow, the song title, and the styling choices. Curiosity continues to grow among global fans about what concept BIGBANG intends to present through this new song and what musical message lies within it.

BIGBANG will release their new digital single [BiiiG] on August 19 at 6 p.m. KST, the date of their 20th debut anniversary. True to the single's title, this marks the return of a K-pop legend that has created a massive (BIG) wave in the music market over the past 20 years. YG previously stated that it is 'the song that most clearly shows where BIGBANG stands today, on the occasion of their 20th debut anniversary,' raising expectations that it will present music that encapsulates their identity.

Following the release, BIGBANG will kick off their 20th debut anniversary world tour 'BIGBANG 2026-2027 WORLD TOUR < XX : COSMOS >' starting in Goyang on August 21, 22, and 23. The massive tour will then span 19 cities worldwide across North America, Europe, Oceania, and Asia, for a total of 33 shows.
