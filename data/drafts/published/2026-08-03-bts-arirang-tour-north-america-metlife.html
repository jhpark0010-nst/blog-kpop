BTS launched the second North America leg of their world tour 'Arirang' on August 1 at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey, near New York City. The group opened the night with the declaration: 'We're back.'

One fan who traveled from New York, an artist named Joan, was wearing a T-shirt she had hand-drawn herself, featuring BTS members and the 'Arirang' stage at Gwanghwamun Square in Seoul. She was also handing out ARMY Bombs, earrings, keychains, and stickers to fellow fans.

Another attendee shared that she had previously been too young to attend concerts. 'I've waited so long, and finally getting to see BTS in person is incredibly moving,' she said.
