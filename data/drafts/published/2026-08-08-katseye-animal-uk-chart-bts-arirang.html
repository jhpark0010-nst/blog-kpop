KATSEYE's new single 'ANIMAL' has entered the top 20 of the UK Official Singles Chart for two consecutive weeks. According to the Official Charts (dated August 7, local time), 'ANIMAL' ranked No. 18 on the Singles Top 100, slipping two spots from the previous week.

Also on the singles chart, 'ICONIC BY MISTAKE' — a collaboration between KATSEYE and fellow HYBE girl groups LE SSERAFIM and ILLIT — debuted at No. 85.

'GOLDEN', the original soundtrack from the Netflix animated film 'K-Pop Demon Hunters', charted at No. 49, marking its 59th consecutive week on the singles chart.

On the albums chart, BTS's fifth studio album 'ARIRANG' rose five spots from the previous week to land at No. 43.
