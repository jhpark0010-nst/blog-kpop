All six members of NCT DREAM have re-signed with SM Entertainment, the agency announced on August 20, 2026. The members confirmed to have renewed their contracts are Haechan, Renjun, Jeno, Jaemin, Chenle, and Jisung.

In its official statement, SM Entertainment said: 'Haechan, who had already completed his contract renewal, along with members Renjun, Jeno, Jaemin, Chenle, and Jisung, have signed new contracts based on their deep trust in one another and their unwavering affection for the team, and will continue their journey with us.'

The agency continued: 'Since their debut on August 25, 2016, NCT DREAM has delivered messages of hope and positivity with their refreshing energy and passion, turning countless dreams into reality over the past 10 years.' It added: 'We sincerely thank the members, who have shared this brilliant journey to becoming icons of growth and leading K-pop artists, and the fans who have always been a great source of strength.'

SM Entertainment further stated: 'We will continue to provide unwavering support so that NCT DREAM's infinite potential can shine even brighter, and we will remain a steadfast partner by their side.'

Meanwhile, NCT DREAM is set to hold their 10th anniversary fan meeting, 'NCT DREAM 10TH ANNIVERSARY PARTY: THE SWEET DREAM HOTEL,' on August 22 and 23 at Inspire Arena in Incheon. SM Entertainment also asked fans to look forward to NCT DREAM's diverse activities continuing in the second half of this year.
