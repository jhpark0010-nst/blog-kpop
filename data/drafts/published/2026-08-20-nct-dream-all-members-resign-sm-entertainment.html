All six members of NCT DREAM have re-signed with their agency SM Entertainment, the label announced on August 20, 2026. The full contract renewal keeps the entire group together as they mark a major milestone in their career.

SM Entertainment stated: 'Haechan, who had already completed his contract renewal, along with members Renjun, Jeno, Jaemin, Chenle, and Jisung, have signed renewed contracts based on their deep trust in one another and their unwavering affection for the team, and will continue their journey with us.'

NCT DREAM debuted in August 2016 and has been beloved for their distinctive music, releasing hit songs including 'Mak-It' (맛), 'Hello Future', 'Buffering', and 'Beatbox' over the course of their career.

NCT, which is celebrating its 10th anniversary this year, had previously seen its sub-unit NCT 127 complete a full contract renewal as well. With NCT DREAM now also achieving a full renewal, the NCT collective continues to maintain its strong bond.

SM Entertainment added: 'NCT DREAM has delivered messages of hope and positive energy with their refreshing energy and passion, turning many dreams into reality over the past 10 years. We sincerely thank the members, who have shared this brilliant journey to become icons of growth and leading K-pop artists, as well as the fans who have been a great source of strength.'
