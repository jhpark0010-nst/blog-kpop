BIGBANG debuted on August 19, 2006, and on their 20th anniversary, the group has returned to the public as a three-member unit — G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung — after weathering numerous storms and member departures over the years.

To mark the milestone, BIGBANG released a new digital single, 'BiiiG,' at 6 p.m. on the 19th. It is their first group release in 4 years and 4 months since 'Still Life' in 2022. Starting August 21 through 23 at Goyang Stadium in Gyeonggi Province, the group will kick off their world tour 'XX : COSMOS,' spanning 19 cities and 33 shows worldwide. A collaboration festival at Hangang Park and a large-scale media exhibition are also being held as part of the 20th anniversary celebrations shared with fans.

Amid the festive atmosphere surrounding BIGBANG's 20th birthday, the bittersweet and contrasting updates on former members who left the group have been drawing a mix of emotions from longtime fans.

First, there is T.O.P (Choi Seung-hyun), who announced his own departure via social media in 2023. T.O.P recently shared a fan account's Reels video on his SNS story, but uploaded it with the word 'BIGBANG' — which appeared in the fan account's username — deliberately blurred out. This stands in stark contrast to the warm gesture shown earlier by the remaining three members, including G-Dragon, who each publicly cheered and gave shout-outs to T.O.P on their own SNS when he released his new solo album. Longtime fans have been unable to hide their disappointment at T.O.P's apparent effort to erase the very name of the group he helped build over the course of 20 years.

The update on Seungri (Lee Seung-hyun), who left the group in disgrace after being identified as a key figure in the 2019 Burning Sun scandal that shook South Korea, is equally grim. A selfie photo showing Seungri's face was recently made public through an ordinary person's SNS account. However, the post attracted a mocking comment reading, 'Is that the famous Defeat?' — a play on his stage name, which means 'victory' in Korean. Though he too has reached the 20th debut anniversary, it highlights how far his public standing has fallen, now reduced to a subject of ridicule.

G-Dragon, Taeyang, and Daesung remain at the center of K-pop after 20 years. Meanwhile, T.O.P appears to be distancing himself by erasing the group's name, and Seungri remains trapped in mockery over irreversible wrongdoing. Though the 20th anniversary is shared in name, the gap between current and former members could not be wider.
