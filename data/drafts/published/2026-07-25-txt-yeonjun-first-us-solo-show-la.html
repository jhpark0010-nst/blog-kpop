TXT member Yeonjun will hold his first solo concert in the United States in Los Angeles on August 12 (local time), his agency Big Hit Music announced on July 25.

The performance will take place as part of 'Billboard: One Night Only,' a special live series hosted by US music media outlet Billboard.

The agency stated, 'The venue will be announced at a later date,' adding, 'As this is Yeonjun's first solo performance in the United States, it will be held at a meaningful location befitting this milestone.'

Yeonjun released his second solo mini-album, 'NO LABELS: PART 02,' this month and has been actively promoting it.
