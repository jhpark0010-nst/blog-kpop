TWICE's Jihyo, who has been with JYP Entertainment for 20 years including her trainee period, shared her complicated feelings with fans on July 28 via a fan platform post.

In her message, Jihyo wrote: 'I could probably just have a casual everyday chat with ONCEs, but my heart is still too heavy to talk so freely right now. I was so worried and careful before reaching out — I'll make sure to sort things out and settle everything quickly so I can share news with ONCEs without making you wait too long.'

She continued: 'As the one in charge of my own life, I'll think things through carefully and wisely before making my choice, so I hope ONCEs will cheer me on and trust me like you always have. I sincerely love and cherish the team called TWICE, and I believe that feeling alone will help ONCEs understand.' She closed by adding: 'I'm sorry it's been so long, and I'll come back when I can have casual conversations and joke around again. Sleep well, eat well, rest well, and let's get through this. Thank you always.'

Jihyo debuted with TWICE in 2015, and including her trainee years, has spent 20 years at JYP Entertainment. Her exclusive contract is nearing its end, and she is reportedly preparing to establish her own one-person agency rather than renewing with JYP.
