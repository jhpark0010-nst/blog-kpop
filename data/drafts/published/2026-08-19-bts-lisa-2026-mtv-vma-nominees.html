BTS, making their comeback after four years, is competing for major awards at the 2026 MTV Video Music Awards (VMAs), one of the four biggest popular music awards shows in the United States. Billboard reported on August 18 (local time) that BTS's 'Swim' has been nominated in two categories: Song of the Year and Best K-pop.

The song 'Golden,' which gained wide popularity as the theme for the animated film 'K-pop Demon Hunters' last year and went on to win Grammy and Oscar awards, has also been nominated for Song of the Year. Song of the Year is one of the VMAs' top prizes, alongside Video of the Year and Artist of the Year.

Among K-pop acts, BLACKPINK's Lisa received the most nominations. Her song 'Dream' was nominated in four categories: Best Pop, Best K-pop, Best Cinematography, and Best Editing. 'Dream' is a track from her first full-length solo album 'Alter Ego,' released last year, and features Japanese actor Kentaro Kyomoto.

CORTIS was nominated for Best New Artist and Best K-pop, while KATSEYE received nominations for Best Choreography and Best K-pop. Additionally, 'Eyes Closed,' a collaboration between BLACKPINK's Jisoo and former One Direction member ZAYN, was nominated for Best Visual Effects. BLACKPINK's 'Jump' and LE SSERAFIM's 'Spaghetti' were also nominated for Best K-pop.

The VMAs, launched in 1984, are a music video-focused awards show hosted annually by MTV, and are considered one of America's four major popular music awards alongside the Grammy Awards, Billboard Music Awards, and American Music Awards (AMAs). Since BTS first received a nomination at the show in 2019 and took home two awards, numerous K-pop acts have won trophies at the ceremony. Last year, BLACKPINK's Rosé became the first K-pop artist to win a top VMA prize, taking Song of the Year.

This year's VMAs are also drawing attention for Madonna's return. Madonna has been nominated in 11 categories, including Artist of the Year and Video of the Year. Madonna is also an iconic figure of the VMAs, having delivered a legendary performance of 'Like a Virgin' in a wedding dress at the very first VMA ceremony in 1984. The awards show is scheduled to be held on September 27 at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles.
