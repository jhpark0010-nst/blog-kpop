Band QWER will launch their second tour, 'ROCKET LAUNCH,' at Korea University's Hwajung Gymnasium in Seoul on September 12-13, 2026, according to an announcement by Three Y Corporation on August 13.

The tour name 'ROCKET LAUNCH' reflects the group's ambition to carry their music to a wider world, like a rocket soaring into space.

Starting with the Seoul dates, QWER will visit nine cities across Asia: Taipei, Taiwan on October 10-11; Fukuoka, Japan on October 13; Osaka, Japan on October 15; Tokyo, Japan on October 17; Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia on November 7; Bangkok, Thailand on November 14; Hong Kong on January 2, 2027; and Singapore on January 8-9, 2027.

In other news, organizer Kakao Entertainment announced that the '2026 Melon Music Awards,' set for November 14-15 at Gocheok Sky Dome in Seoul, will introduce a new 'Global Top 10' category. The category was created to communicate more closely with global K-pop fans. It will be based on the 'Global-K Chart,' which combines music streaming data from Korea's Melon, China's Tencent Music, and Japan's Line Music, along with fan activity metrics. Like the existing 'Top 10,' it is a main award, selecting 10 artists who have received great love worldwide. A total of 30 nominees will be chosen based on cumulative chart scores, with final winners determined by chart score (60%), judging (20%), and fan voting (20%).

Meanwhile, musician Sumin will release her second full-length album, 'dinnermode,' at 6 p.m. on August 13, as announced by Standard Friends. This marks her first full album in eight years since 2018. She has collaborated with various artists including IU, Kiki, and Red Velvet in the interim. 'dinnermode' is an album capturing love and boredom, jokes and silence — the moments between special events — and was fully produced by Sumin herself.
