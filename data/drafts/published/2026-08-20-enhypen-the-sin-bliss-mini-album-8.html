ENHYPEN held a press conference on August 20 at Mondrian Seoul Itaewon in Yongsan-gu, Seoul, to celebrate the release of their 8th mini album 'THE SIN : BLISS'. The event was hosted by MC Shin A-young. It marks the group's first album release as a six-member unit following their lineup change, and their first release since the group's rebranding.

The members shared their feelings about the comeback, saying: 'We are back after seven months. We have prepared songs that are incredibly cool and full of energy. We can't wait to show EN-GENEs (the fandom name) our new side.' Jay in particular expressed strong confidence: 'This album was made while preparing for the world tour and various other schedules, and I am proud to say it is ENHYPEN's best album.'

ENHYPEN had previously reorganized as a six-member group following member Heeseung's departure in March. Jungwon emphasized, 'We are always of one mind,' adding: 'We focused on how to make the best possible result rather than on the number of members, and we have always put the stage and performance first no matter the circumstances. Through this tour, we gained a lot of energy from EN-GENEs, and thanks to that we were able to prepare well for the comeback.' Jungwon also noted: 'There was a concert in Australia just two or three days after the team was reorganized, and we had to take the stage professionally. Staying busy the whole time, we held on by thinking about the album and our fans.'

Sunghoon shared: 'Even before the rebranding, we had the keyword of connection. Through this opportunity, we donated blood and engaged in various activities that connected us with many people. Going forward, we want to be an ENHYPEN that spreads a positive influence.'

The new album is the second installment in the 'THE : SIN' series, depicting the conclusion of a journey faced by lovers who break taboos and choose to flee. It includes a total of 9 tracks: the title track 'Bloody Paradise', 'Two Fools', 'Breaking News' (속보), 'Stuck', 'Bad For You', 'Remnants of Love' (사랑의 잔해), 'The Final Moment' (최후의 순간), 'Checkmate', 'Highlight', and 'Hidden Truth' (가려진 진실). The album is structured in the format of a mystery show reporting on a vampire escape incident. Actor Park Jung-min serves as host, and MBC news anchor announcer Kim Su-ji personally wrote the breaking news reporting script and appears as narrator.

Jay introduced the album, saying: 'If you listen to the music tracks in order, it is an album that draws you deeply into the story we want to tell.' He added: 'We will promote with the conviction that this will be the most brilliant album, as it is the one we are most satisfied with.' He further stated: 'As people who perform music and put on performances, we monitor content such as performances and music videos for every album. For this album, it was unprecedented — everyone unanimously agreed the quality was high. In every aspect, this is the album we have prepared with the least regrets out of all our albums so far.'

The title track captures, in vivid contrast to a desperate situation, the inner world of lovers — just as its paradoxical title suggests. The genre is Brazilian funk, and Julia Lewis, who produced Bad Bunny's Grammy Award-winning track 'DIMF', participated in the songwriting. Sunghoon said: 'I felt this title track is different from everything we have done before. We released a members' remix of the 7th mini album's title track separately, and that was when we first tried the Brazilian funk genre.' He continued: 'It felt fresh and fun that a genre I personally enjoy listening to became the title. The way I see it, tteokbokki (spicy rice cakes) is food you can easily find and eat anywhere, but it is also very bold and stimulating — and I got the feeling that our song is like tteokbokki eaten at a restaurant. If you listen with that thought in mind, you will enjoy it even more.'

Jake said: 'Except for Sunghoon, it is a first for all of us, so it feels different, but at the same time I thought it was an opportunity to show a new side of ourselves. We also have a dome tour scheduled in Japan, and since we are doing so many tours, our goal is to perform on even bigger stages.' Ni-ki explained: 'I sharpened my teeth preparing this album. It is a sound that is trending these days, but since it is our first time trying it, I think people will be able to feel both trendiness and freshness at the same time.'

On ENHYPEN's unique strengths, the members said: 'When we heard we would be doing the Brazilian funk genre, many members already had an affection for it and we knew there was potential, so we had high expectations. We thought a lot about what only we could offer, and the keyword is concept.' They added: 'Fans have long told us that ENHYPEN is the only artist that puts this much effort into concept and builds out a storyline. This is an album where we worked hard to meet those expectations and make the most of our strengths. We are confident we can express it in ENHYPEN's own color.'

When asked about goals, the members said: 'There are still so many places we haven't been, so as long as EN-GENEs are there, we want to go anywhere and perform. We are also hoping for good results on the Billboard charts.' Jay reflected on the members' growth during the album preparation process: 'We are carrying out so many stages and schedules, and through that process I feel the members have grown in many ways. I already knew ENHYPEN was a strong team, but seeing them pull through in an even more solid and bold manner, I definitely come to see them as icons who grow more with every passing moment.' He concluded: 'Each member has gone through their own worries and efforts to get here, and recent activities have made me look back on that.'

ENHYPEN's 8th mini album 'THE SIN : BLISS' is set to be released on August 21.
