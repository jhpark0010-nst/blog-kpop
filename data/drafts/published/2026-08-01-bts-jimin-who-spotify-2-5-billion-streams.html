BTS Jimin's title track 'WHO' from his second solo album MUSE has surpassed 2.5 billion streams on Spotify, Big Hit Music announced on August 1. The milestone was reached on July 30, making 'WHO' Jimin's most-streamed solo track of all time.

'WHO' is a hip-hop R&B song released in July 2024 that captures a longing and emotional confusion for someone the narrator has never met.

The track previously peaked at No. 12 on the Billboard Hot 100 (dated August 3, 2024) and remained on the chart for a total of 33 weeks.

Meanwhile, BTS is currently conducting their world tour 'ARIRANG'.
