TWS has topped the Oricon chart in Japan. According to the latest Oricon chart (dated August 3) announced on the 4th, TWS's second Japanese single 'SODA SODA' sold 161,253 copies on its first day of release, claiming the number one spot on the Daily Singles Ranking.

This figure surpasses the approximately 150,000 copies sold during the entire first week of their Japanese debut single 'Nice to see you again' (original Japanese title: はじめまして), which was released last year — and TWS achieved this in just a single day.

The title track 'SODA SODA' also entered Japan's LINE MUSIC real-time Song Top 100, and ranked 12th on the K-Pop Top 100 as of August 3.

TWS is a six-member multinational boy group under Pledis Entertainment that debuted on January 22, 2024. The group's name stands for 'TWENTY FOUR SEVEN WITH US,' carrying the meaning of being together every moment of every hour of the day and every day of the week.
