BTS member Jimin's shirt, worn during the halftime performance at the 2026 North America World Cup final, sold for $110,000 (approximately 159.06 million Korean won) — the highest price achieved in the auction.

According to a July 29 report by The New York Times, the online charity auction titled 'One Goal: Auction for the FIFA Global Citizen Education Fund,' organized by Christie's in the United States, concluded after running for seven days.

The auction raised a total of $652,630 (approximately 943.7 million Korean won), far exceeding initial expectations.

Christie's reported that bidders from 24 countries around the world participated in the online auction. Notably, seven items worn by BTS members during their halftime performance of 'Dynamite' sold for a combined total of $300,300 (approximately 434.32 million Korean won), accounting for 46% of the total auction proceeds.

The BTS items included a scarf worn by V ($46,200), a jacket worn by Jungkook ($46,200), pants worn by Suga ($33,000), gloves worn by J-Hope ($28,600), boots worn by RM ($19,800), and a belt worn by Jin ($16,500). Among these, Jimin's outfit fetched $110,000 and, along with the match ball used in the final, tied for the highest price in the entire auction.

Other notable lots included a 2026 Argentina home jersey signed by Lionel Messi and gifted to Shakira ($66,000), fingerless goalkeeper gloves worn and signed by Madonna during the opening performance ($41,800), and 'Skylrk' sneakers worn by Justin Bieber during his acoustic performance of 'Everything Hallelujah' ($26,400).

All proceeds from the auction will go to the FIFA Global Citizen Education Fund, which aims to raise $100 million to expand access to quality education and football participation for children in underserved communities.
