ENHYPEN is holding their world tour 'BLOOD SAGA' at Busan Sajik Indoor Gymnasium on August 8 and 9, according to an announcement from Belift Lab.

The Busan concerts are part of the comeback promotion for ENHYPEN's 8th mini album 'THE SIN : BLISS', which is set for release on the 21st.

The concerts will feature spatial design and song selections that reflect ENHYPEN's vampire concept.

At this performance, the stage for the new album's title track 'Bloody Paradise' will be revealed to the public for the very first time.

'Bloody Paradise' is a Brazilian funk dance track that portrays the situation of two lovers who, even amid crisis, focus only on each other right in front of them, expressing that moment as a paradise.
