EXO will hold an encore concert at the KSPO Dome in Olympic Park, Songpa-gu, Seoul on November 6–8, 2026. SM Entertainment announced on August 20 that the concerts will be part of the group's sixth tour, 'EXO PLANET #6 - EXhOrizon dot -'.

EXO kicked off the tour in Seoul in April and has since performed 27 shows across 14 regions around the world.

According to their agency, the encore concerts will feature a more advanced version of the stage compared to the previous tour dates.

In other news, SEVENTEEN's 'HOT' has surpassed 300 million streams on Spotify, the world's largest music streaming platform, Pledis Entertainment announced. The track recorded 300,002,304 streams on the platform as of August 18. This makes 'HOT' the second SEVENTEEN song to cross the 300 million stream mark, following 'Son Oh Gong'.

'HOT' is a hip-hop track from SEVENTEEN's fourth full-length album 'Face the Sun', released in 2022. The song expresses the will to boldly face the blazing sun and ultimately become that sun oneself.
