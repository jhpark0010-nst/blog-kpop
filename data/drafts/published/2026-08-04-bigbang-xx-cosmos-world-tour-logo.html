BIGBANG has officially launched their large-scale world tour celebrating their 20th debut anniversary. On August 4, YG Entertainment revealed the logo motion for the 'BIGBANG 2026-2027 WORLD TOUR XX : COSMOS' through the group's official social media channels.

Following the previously announced tour title, this logo motion marks the first content to unveil the unique aura and universe that the tour will bring to life. Trails of light cutting through pitch-black darkness blend with a heavy soundscape to deliver an overwhelming sense of immersion reminiscent of a sci-fi film trailer. In particular, an infinity symbol (∞) rendered in intricately interlocking threads captures the eye instantly, expressing the vast order of the cosmos through striking visuals.

The logo encapsulates BIGBANG's universe as embedded in the tour title 'XX : COSMOS.' The infinity-derived shape carries two simultaneous meanings: '20' representing the 20th anniversary of their debut, and an endlessly expanding sense of infinity. The addition of 'COSMOS' — signifying connection, relationship, and continuity — completes a single unified world that reflects the 20-year bond BIGBANG has built with fans and the new journey ahead.

BIGBANG is a pioneering act that has opened new frontiers for K-pop performances in the global music market. Beginning with the 'ALIVE GALAXY' tour, which expanded K-pop's reach worldwide, the group has set unprecedented records: the most audience members for a single K-pop artist tour (1.5 million), the first overseas artist to complete a 6-dome Japan tour and 5 consecutive years of dome tours, the most audience and most cities in China (250,000 attendees across 13 cities), and the largest-scale North American tour.

This world tour, realized approximately nine years after their last, holds special significance as the stage that opens a new chapter for BIGBANG at their 20th anniversary. Drawing on two decades of accumulated musical talent and performance expertise, the group is expected to deliver an evolved performance of high quality that will give fans unforgettable moments.

BIGBANG will hold 'BIGBANG 2026-2027 WORLD TOUR XX : COSMOS IN GOYANG presented by Coupang Play' at Goyang Stadium for three days, from the 21st to the 23rd. All three shows — including additional restricted-view seats — have sold out. The tour will span a total of 19 cities and 33 shows, with further dates and venues to be announced at a later date.
