BLACKPINK has unveiled the 'BLACKPINK Heritage Collection,' a collaboration with Muyds, the official merchandise brand of Korea's National Museum. YG Plus announced the release on August 5. The collection was created to mark BLACKPINK's 10th debut anniversary.

The collaboration captures the journey and accumulated symbolism of BLACKPINK's decade-long career through a series of objects reinterpreted with a contemporary sensibility. The lineup includes seven product types such as fans and combs. Starting August 8, the items will also be displayed in a section of the permanent exhibition hall at the National Museum of Korea.

TXT member Yeonjun will throw the ceremonial first pitch at a Major League Baseball game, Big Hit Music announced. He is set to appear as the first-pitch thrower at the Los Angeles Dodgers vs. Milwaukee Brewers game at Dodger Stadium in Los Angeles on August 13 (local time). BTS member V and soccer player Son Heung-min have previously drawn attention for throwing first pitches at the same venue.

Yeonjun is also scheduled to perform at the 'Korean Cultural Heritage Night' event held on the same day. 'Korean Cultural Heritage Night' is a signature annual cultural event organized by the Los Angeles Dodgers.

(G)I-DLE's music video for 'Nxde' has surpassed 400 million views on YouTube, Cube Entertainment announced. 'Nxde' is the title track from (G)I-DLE's fifth mini album 'I love,' released in October 2022, and uses the word 'nude' as a metaphor for one's unadorned true self.

This marks the second time a (G)I-DLE music video has reached 400 million views, following 'Queencard.' (G)I-DLE will hold a solo concert at Galaxy Arena in Macau on August 29 and 30.
