SEVENTEEN member Vernon has released a new single. On August 20 at 6 p.m. KST, Vernon dropped his new track 'I'll Be Back' across major online music streaming platforms.

'I'll Be Back' is an electronic track with a strong pop sensibility. The song was first performed live in July at the '2026 VERNON THE 8 [V8] LIVE' concert. Vernon participated in writing the lyrics and composing the music, embedding a message for his fans.

Vernon has released a variety of solo tracks over the years, including 'Mia' from the V8 mini album Vol. 1, the solo track 'Shining Star' included on SEVENTEEN's fifth full-length album, and the mixtape 'Black Eye.' He has been steadily continuing his music work alongside THE 8 on the V8 album project, as well as collaborating with artists both in Korea and abroad.

Vernon joined the Recording Academy as a voting member eligible to participate in selecting winners for the 2025 U.S. Grammy Awards, and in 2024 he was elevated to full membership in the Korea Music Copyright Association.

Vernon, who debuted with SEVENTEEN in 2015, entered the military training center on August 20, the same day as his new single's release. After completing basic military training, he is scheduled to fulfill his service as a social service worker (social service personnel). His expected discharge date is May 19, 2028.
