ATEEZ has landed on the main U.S. Billboard chart for four consecutive weeks. According to the latest chart published by Billboard on July 28 (local time), ATEEZ's 14th mini album 'GOLDEN HOUR: Part.5', released on June 26, entered the Billboard 200 (dated August 1) at No. 121.

The album also climbed higher than the previous week on both the World Albums chart and the Top Album Sales chart.

In its first week of release, ATEEZ had debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 (dated July 11) with the same 14th mini album, breaking their own record for highest first-week U.S. sales. At the same time, they set the record for the most Billboard 200 Top 10 entries among K-pop groups in the 2020s.

ATEEZ is an eight-member boy group under KQ Entertainment that debuted in 2018. The group consists of Hongjoong, Seonghwa, Yunho, Yeosang, San, Mingi, Wooyoung, and Jongho.
