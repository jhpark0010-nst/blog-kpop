SM Entertainment announced on August 5 that it achieved strong results in Q2 2026, driven by active overseas tours and growth in merchandise (MD) business. As part of the announcement, SM confirmed that Girls' Generation unit 'Girls' Generation-HRS' (효리수) will release a single in Q3 2026, drawing significant attention from fans.

SM disclosed through its earnings release that consolidated revenue for Q2 2026 reached 349.6 billion KRW, with operating profit of 52.9 billion KRW. These figures represent year-on-year increases of 15.4% and 11.0%, respectively.

On a standalone basis, Q2 revenue rose 9.2% year-on-year to 240.6 billion KRW. Driven by expanded global tours from major artists including TVXQ, Super Junior, EXO, aespa, and NCT WISH, performance revenue grew 27.9% and concert revenue grew 23.6%. MD and licensing revenue also climbed 22.0%, boosted by large-scale concert merchandise and pop-up store activations.

Subsidiaries also posted notable results. Dreammaker significantly improved profitability through expanded collaboration with the parent company and increased domestic concert production, while DearU maintained steady profit growth thanks to subscription fee adjustments and a shift to web-based payments.

Among the Q3 lineup, the Girls' Generation unit 'Girls' Generation-HRS' — composed of members Hyoyeon, Yuri, and Sooyoung — is set to release a new song, showcasing a distinct chemistry. Also scheduled for Q3 are a Red Velvet mini album (released August 3), a full-length album from NCT 127, and mini albums from Minho and WayV. In Q4, full-length albums from Yesung, Taeyeon, Jaehyun, and NCT DREAM are planned, along with mini albums from Choi Siwon (Choikang Changmin), Xiaojun, and NCT WISH, plus a single from Hearts2Hearts.

On the concert front, aespa will kick off a new global tour at Seoul's Gocheok Sky Dome on August 7 and 8. World tours from U-Know Yunho, Super Junior-83z, Yesung, Ryeowook, NCT 127, and WayV, as well as an NCT WISH fan meeting tour, are also set to roll out actively.
