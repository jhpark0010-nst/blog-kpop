OURBIRTHDAY has made their fresh debut in the K-pop scene. On August 19 at 6 PM, the full audio for OURBIRTHDAY's first single album 'Our Birthday' and the music video for their title track 'SQUEEZY' were released.

'Our Birthday' is an album that vividly captures the group's signature genre 'WHIP-HOP' — a blend of the seven members' individual personalities coming together like whipped cream, combined with dynamic hip-hop performance.

The title track 'SQUEEZY' is a funk-based dance song featuring a highly addictive melody that stays in your head after just one listen. Like squeezing a squishy toy, the song delivers a message of shaking off complicated emotions and everyday stress, expressed through the seven members' witty and bouncy energy.

OURBIRTHDAY is the new girl group launched by JYP Entertainment approximately four years after NMIXX, which drew intense anticipation from global K-pop fans even before their debut. True to their ambition of savoring free and thrilling moments as if every day were a birthday, the group is expected to bring fresh energy to the music scene with their one-of-a-kind music and performances.
