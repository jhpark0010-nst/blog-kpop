Red Velvet will unveil the stage for their new song for the first time at their upcoming fan concert. The group will hold '2026 Red Velvet FAN-CON: A Day in Red & Velvet' at Hwajeong Gymnasium, Korea University in Seoul, running from July 31 to August 2.

During the concert, the title track 'Surfin' Boy' from their summer mini album — set for release on August 3 — will be performed live for the very first time.

Red Velvet will take the stage alongside dancers to deliver a performance capturing the languid mood of summer. In particular, the chorus section featuring 'surfin' boy' will showcase point choreography inspired by the Hawaiian hand gesture known as the 'shaka,' as well as wave-like movements that ripple like gentle ocean swells.

Meanwhile, Red Velvet is set to release their summer mini album 'Velvet Summer' on August 3. This album marks their first full-group release in approximately two years, since 'Cosmic' in June 2024.
