NCT WISH has received their first ever Platinum certification from the Recording Industry Association of Japan (RIAJ). According to the RIAJ, the group's Japanese single 'YO-I-DON! / BOY MEETS GIRL,' released on July 15, surpassed 250,000 cumulative copies shipped as of July, earning a Platinum certification in the Gold Disc category. The announcement was made on August 10.

At the time of the single's release, NCT WISH debuted at No. 1 on the Oricon Daily Singles Chart. The double title track 'YO-I-DON!' also climbed to No. 3 on the Billboard Japan Hot 100, achieving strong results in both physical and digital sales. With this first Platinum certification added to their record, the group has further proven their popularity in Japan.

NCT WISH is a six-member multinational boy group under SM Entertainment that debuted in February 2024. The group consists of Sion, Riku, Yuushi, Jaehee, Ryo, and Sakuya.
