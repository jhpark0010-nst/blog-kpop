HYBE girl group KATSEYE and boy group CORTIS have been named to Billboard's '2026 21 Under 21' list, which spotlights young leaders under the age of 21. Billboard announced this year's selections on July 31 (local time) via its official website.

The annual Billboard feature highlights 21 next-generation artists, taking into account quantitative metrics such as album and song sales, streaming figures, social media reach, and radio and TV audience numbers, as well as overall industry impact and growth trajectory.

KATSEYE appeared on the list for the second consecutive year. Billboard noted that this has been the group's biggest year since debut, marked by appearances at major global festivals and nominations in two categories at the Grammy Awards.

In an interview with Billboard, KATSEYE shared their mindset toward their work: 'You cannot succeed unless you truly want it with your whole heart.'

CORTIS made the list for the first time. Billboard described CORTIS as one of the fastest-growing acts on the Billboard charts.

The members shared their feelings in an interview, saying, 'This was something we had dreamed of for a long time, and experiencing it in reality hit even harder.' They added, 'Through these experiences, we felt that our music is reaching more people beyond where it started, and we were truly grateful.'
