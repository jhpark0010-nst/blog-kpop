BTS continues its long-running Billboard success with 'SWIM'. According to the latest chart dated August 15, released by Billboard on the 11th, 'SWIM' — the title track of BTS's fifth full-length album ARIRANG — entered the Billboard main song chart Hot 100 at No. 91.

'SWIM' first debuted at No. 1 on the Hot 100 dated April 4, and has now maintained a chart position for 20 consecutive weeks. This ties the total Hot 100 chart run of 'Butter', the mega-hit released in 2021, which also logged 20 weeks on the chart.

The song's strength on the global song charts — which tally streaming and digital sales across more than 200 countries and regions worldwide — is also notable. 'SWIM' ranked No. 4 on the Global (Excl. U.S.) chart and No. 12 on the Global 200. Particularly striking is its 20 consecutive weeks in the top 10 on the Global (Excl. U.S.) chart, underscoring BTS's influence that is not limited to any single region.

'NORMAL' also ranked at No. 24 and No. 49 on the two respective global charts. The track has been regaining presence on the Billboard charts since its music video and Korean-language audio were released on the 17th of last month. It also appeared on the radio chart Pop Airplay at No. 40.

A total of six tracks from ARIRANG — 'SWIM', 'NORMAL', 'Body to Body', '2.0', 'Hooligan', and 'Aliens' — all appeared on the Global (Excl. U.S.) chart. Additionally, 'Dynamite', a 2020 release performed at the halftime show of the 2026 FIFA World Cup final in July, has now charted on that same chart for three consecutive weeks.

On the main album chart Billboard 200, ARIRANG continued its steady popularity, rebounding from No. 15 last week to No. 14 this week. The album previously topped the Billboard 200 for three consecutive weeks, and its 13 cumulative weeks in the top 10 is the longest of any Korean artist's album. This week's detailed chart figures are No. 7 on Top Album Sales, No. 25 on Top Streaming Albums, and No. 11 on Vinyl Albums.

Meanwhile, 'I NEED U' — the title track of BTS's 2015 mini album 'The Most Beautiful Moment in Life, Pt. 1' — surpassed 500 million cumulative streams on Spotify as of August 8. The song was the one that earned BTS their first No. 1 on a terrestrial music broadcast (music show). It blends lyrical Eastern sentiment with a raw hip-hop sound, capturing the desperate longing to hold on to a love that is coming to an end.
