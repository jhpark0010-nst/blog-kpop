Boy group WHIB is maintaining a rapid upward trajectory, taking on a series of major projects in quick succession — a Japan digital single release, a European festival appearance, and a domestic comeback. Their agency, CJS Studio, announced that WHIB (Kim Junmin, Ha Seung, Jinbeom, Yugeon, Leejung, Jaeha, Wonjun) officially released their first Japanese digital single, 'First Love Never Die,' on July 29 at 6 p.m. KST across major global music platforms.

The single is an original track written, composed, and arranged entirely by member Leejung. It is available in two versions — Japanese and English — as part of the group's push into the global market.

WHIB has also been invited to perform at 'K-POP DAYS,' a large-scale K-pop festival held in Romania on August 1 and 2. This marks the group's first European stage since their debut, expanding their reach beyond the Asian market and expected to leave a strong impression on international fans.

Alongside their overseas schedule, WHIB is also accelerating preparations for their domestic comeback. On July 28, they unveiled the 'MIDNIGHT' version of their second concept photo for mini album 'CHERRY PIE,' raising anticipation among fans in Korea.

WHIB's first Japanese digital single 'First Love Never Die' was officially released on July 29, while their second mini album 'CHERRY PIE' is set to drop on August 5 at 6 p.m. KST across various music platforms. The seven-member group, who debuted in 2023, previously drew attention with releases including 'BANG!' and 'KICK IT.'
