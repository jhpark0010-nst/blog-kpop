Singer and musical actress IVY shared her candid feelings ahead of her Broadway debut, set to begin on August 17 (local time). On August 10, she posted a short video on her social media account with the caption: 'Still haven't gotten to see New York. Planning to do that after all the performances are done.'

The video showed IVY taking a walk through the streets of New York with her dog Dudu. She added honestly, 'I'm not enjoying any of it because of the pressure,' conveying just how nervous she is ahead of the big moment.

IVY also shared a photo with a well-known choreographer, writing alongside it: 'Someone who always tells me, "You deserve this." I need to keep telling myself the same thing. Enjoy yourself, me.'

IVY will take the stage as Roxie Hart in the Broadway production of the musical 'Chicago' from August 17 (local time) through September 6. Having performed the role of Roxie Hart in the Korean production of 'Chicago' nearly 600 times, she received an audition offer from the Broadway production last year and, after rigorous preparation, is finally stepping onto her dream stage.

It is extremely rare for a musical actress based in Korea to be cast as a lead in a Broadway production. At the time, IVY shared her thoughts: 'There were three video auditions and a long wait over the course of a year. That is how precious this opportunity is to me, and I also feel a heavy sense of responsibility. I will prepare well, with the mindset of representing the Republic of Korea.'

IVY debuted as a singer in 2005 with her first album 'My Sweet And Free Day' and went on to release hit songs including 'Sonata of Temptation,' 'If You Were Going to Be Like This,' 'I Must Be a Fool,' 'A-HA,' and 'Goodbye Tears.' In addition to 'Chicago,' she has appeared in numerous major musicals such as 'Wicked,' 'Aida,' 'Red Book,' and 'Moulin Rouge!'
