BTS's hit song 'IDOL' has surpassed 1.4 billion views on YouTube, their label Big Hit Music announced on July 30, 2026. The music video crossed the 1.4 billion mark at approximately 9:20 PM the previous evening. This makes 'IDOL' the fifth BTS music video to reach the milestone, following 'Dynamite,' 'Boy With Luv,' 'DNA,' and the 'MIC DROP' remix.

'IDOL' is the title track of BTS's repackaged album 'LOVE YOURSELF 結 Answer,' released in August 2018. At the time of its release, the song was described as 'Joseon EDM (Electronic Dance Music)' for its blend of South African beats with traditional Korean rhythms and vocal exclamations. Interjections such as 'Eolssu jota, jihwaja jota, deonggi-deo kungdeoreoreo' combined with African rhythms to create a track that was both distinctly Korean and globally appealing.

The song is also included in the setlist of BTS's ongoing world tour 'ARIRANG,' the largest-scale K-pop world tour currently underway, which began in April.

The 'IDOL' music video is noted for its striking visuals, featuring sets inspired by tropical savanna landscapes, the Bukchong Lion Dance, Euro-Asian architecture, and traditional Korean aesthetics. The choreography blends the African dance style guaraguara with Korean traditional dance.

The music video won 'Music Video of the Year' at the 2018 E! People's Choice Awards in the United States, which covers music, film, and television.
