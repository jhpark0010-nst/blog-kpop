KATSEYE, the Korea-U.S. joint girl group under HYBE, released their 3rd mini album 'WILD' at midnight on August 14, according to HYBE and Geffen Records.

'WILD' is an album that captures KATSEYE embracing every aspect of their inner selves as they are, moving toward instinct and freedom.

The title track 'Hootie Frutti' is characterized by a rich pop sound and heavy drums.

The album contains a total of five tracks, including pre-released singles 'PINKY UP' and 'Animal'.

HYBE Chairman Bang Si-hyuk and pop star Ed Sheeran, along with renowned producers Omer Fedi and Blake Slatkin, participated in the songwriting and production.

Ahead of their comeback, KATSEYE appeared on NBC's 'The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon' on August 13 (local time), performing 'Animal'.
