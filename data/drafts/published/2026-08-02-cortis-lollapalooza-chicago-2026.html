CORTIS performed a total of 11 songs over approximately 40 minutes at Lollapalooza Chicago on August 1 (local time), according to their agency Big Hit Music on August 2. The group took the stage at Grant Park in Chicago for one of the year's biggest music festivals.

CORTIS opened their set on the T-MOBILE stage with 'GO!' and 'What You Want'. The 50,000-strong crowd was already chanting 'CORTIS' before the performance began, and the cheers grew louder each time the five members came closer to the audience.

The members performed a string of fan-favorite tracks including 'REDRED', 'ACAI', and 'YOUNGCREATORCREW', heating up the atmosphere throughout the set. Big Hit Music noted that 'CORTIS moved all across the stage with intense headbanging and formation changes,' adding that 'their vocals never wavered despite the vigorous movements.' During the remix version of 'TNT', the members even stepped down from the stage to the floor to connect with fans up close.

CORTIS also shared a joint stage with Southern hip-hop star Juicy J, performing their recently released new song 'MOTION' together. CORTIS referred to Juicy J as 'big brother' to show their respect, while Juicy J put his arms around the members' shoulders to express his closeness with the group.

The concert was livestreamed on the fan platform Weverse, and on Disney+ and Hulu for audiences in the United States, Japan, and Latin America. Through Weverse Live, approximately 167,000 viewers from 185 countries including South Korea, China, the United Kingdom, Germany, and Canada tuned in.

CORTIS shared through their agency: 'We were truly happy to be part of Lollapalooza Chicago, a stage we have dreamed of since we were young. The passionate performance atmosphere we had only vaguely imagined while working on our second mini-album GREENGREEN became a reality, and we felt overwhelmed and happy.' CORTIS is set to kick off their first tour, 'PUT YOUR PHONE DOWN', in Toronto, Canada on August 4, visiting six cities across North America. This year's Lollapalooza Chicago lineup also included other K-pop stars such as BLACKPINK's Jennie, (G)I-DLE, and aespa.
