ONEUS is set to embark on their first world tour since changing agencies. On the 30th, the group revealed the first poster for the '2026 ONEUS WORLD TOUR FULL MOON' through their official accounts, announcing the upcoming tour.

According to the released poster, ONEUS will kick off the world tour in Seoul on October 24 and 25. The tour will then expand to South America, Europe, North America, and Asia.

This world tour marks approximately one year since their last tour, and is the first tour ONEUS has undertaken since moving to a new agency.

Meanwhile, ONEUS is a five-member boy group consisting of Seoho, Leedo, Keonhee, Hwanwoong, and Xion. They debuted in 2019 with their first mini album 'LIGHT US' and are celebrating their 7th debut anniversary this year.
