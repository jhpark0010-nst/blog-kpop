SEVENTEEN has added another 300-million-stream song to their Spotify catalog. According to the global music streaming platform Spotify, 'HOT,' the title track from SEVENTEEN's fourth studio album Face the Sun, recorded a cumulative play count of 300,020,304 streams as of August 18.

With this achievement, SEVENTEEN now holds their second Spotify song to surpass 300 million streams, following 'Sonsogu' (Son Oh Gong).

'HOT' is a hip-hop track that incorporates a western guitar sound, carrying the message of bravely facing a blazing sun and ultimately becoming that sun oneself. SEVENTEEN member Woozi participated in writing and composing the song alongside singer-producer BUMZU.

Meanwhile, SEVENTEEN is a 13-member multinational boy group under Pledis Entertainment who debuted on May 26, 2015. Last month, all members signed their second contract renewal with the agency.
