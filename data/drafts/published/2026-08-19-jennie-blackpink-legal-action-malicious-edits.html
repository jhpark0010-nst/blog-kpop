BLACKPINK's Jennie has announced legal action against malicious online posts targeting her. On August 19, her agency OA Entertainment released an official statement addressing the ongoing situation.

OA Entertainment stated: 'We have continuously confirmed the spread of groundless defamation and false information about our artist Jennie across online communities and social media, as well as acts of maliciously editing or manipulating the artist's photos and videos, and posting or distributing content that violates her privacy and personal rights.'

The agency added: 'We are taking these violations of our artist's rights very seriously, and in cooperation with professional legal representatives, we are continuing to monitor related posts and accounts and collect evidence, while civil and criminal legal proceedings are currently underway for serious cases of rights infringement.'

OA Entertainment further specified: 'In particular, acts of maliciously editing or re-processing the artist's physical appearance, repeatedly posting, reposting, or distributing specific scenes from photos or videos, and acts of attaching insulting expressions or false information to such content will be subject to legal response according to the nature and severity of each case.'
