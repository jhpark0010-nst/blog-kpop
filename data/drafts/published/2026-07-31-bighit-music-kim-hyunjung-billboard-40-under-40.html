Big Hit Music co-CEO Kim Hyunjung has been named to Billboard's '2026 Billboard 40 Under 40,' a list recognizing next-generation leaders under the age of 40 who have achieved outstanding results across various sectors of the music industry.

On July 30 (local time), Billboard's official website introduced Kim as 'the person overseeing the operations and business of BTS,' noting that she 'successfully led the launch of BTS's comeback album ARIRANG, released in March, and their stadium tour.'

Kim Hyunjung is the only Korean to be selected for the '2026 Billboard 40 Under 40.' In an interview with Billboard, she said, 'The moment ARIRANG was released is one of my proudest achievements.' She also stated, 'The past 12 months have been filled with countless historic moments for BTS, and I am deeply grateful to have been part of that journey.'

The 'Billboard 40 Under 40' is a list that spotlights next-generation leaders under 40 who have achieved remarkable results across diverse areas including artists, labels, platforms, management, and the live business.
