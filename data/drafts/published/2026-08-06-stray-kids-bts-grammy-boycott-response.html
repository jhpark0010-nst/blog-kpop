Stray Kids spoke about the Grammy Awards submission controversy at a press conference held on the afternoon of August 6 at the Conrad Seoul Grand Ballroom Hall in Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul. The event was held to celebrate the release of the group's new mini album 'THIS & THAT,' with all eight members — Bang Chan, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, Han, Felix, Seungmin, and I.N — in attendance.

Recently, BTS announced they would not be submitting to the Grammy Awards after the Recording Academy introduced a new category called 'Best Asian Pop Music Performance.' As a result, Stray Kids emerged as a leading candidate for the new category, and the members shared their thoughts on the matter.

Seungmin said, 'When we talk about the music we love or discuss our performances, our gaze is not always directed outward — we create stages, make music, and write lyrics from what comes from within us. Rather than feeling external pressure, we keep pouring in the colors and stories we want to tell.' He added, 'We feel so grateful that K-pop as a major genre is receiving such great love around the world. It feels like we are reminded once again of the power of music. We are continuing to walk forward and make sure the music we write reaches as many people as possible.'

Hyunjin also emphasized, 'Not once since our activities as Stray Kids have I ever set award results or records as a goal when releasing an album. With this album as well, I want to show and prove to as many people as possible what 'THIS & THAT' — an album where we can do all kinds of things — is about. That is our biggest goal and dream, so making our fans satisfied and happy is our top priority.'

Meanwhile, Stray Kids will release their new mini album 'THIS & THAT' on August 7 at 1 p.m., along with the title track 'This & That.'
