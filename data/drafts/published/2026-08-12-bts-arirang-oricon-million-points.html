BTS's fifth full-length album ARIRANG achieved 1 million cumulative points on Japan's Oricon 'Combined Album Ranking' on August 12, 2026, according to Big Hit Music. The milestone makes ARIRANG the third BTS album to surpass this threshold on the chart.

Oricon's 'Combined Album Ranking' calculates points by converting physical album sales, digital downloads, and streaming figures into a single combined score.

With this achievement, BTS now holds three albums that have each surpassed 1 million Oricon points: the Japanese best album BTS, THE BEST; the Japanese fourth studio album MAP OF THE SOUL : 7 ~ THE JOURNEY ~; and now ARIRANG.

This record makes BTS only the third act in Oricon history to accomplish this feat, following Japanese artists Snow Man and Mrs. GREEN APPLE. BTS is the only foreign artist to have done so.

Additionally, Billboard Japan reported that 'Dynamite,' BTS's digital single released in 2020, has surpassed 1 billion cumulative streams in Japan. The figure combines stream counts from major Japanese music platforms including Amazon Music, Spotify, and LINE MUSIC, as well as YouTube Music view data provided by global music data company Luminate.

'Dynamite' is the first foreign song to surpass 1 billion streams in Japan, and it is also the most-streamed BTS single in the country.
