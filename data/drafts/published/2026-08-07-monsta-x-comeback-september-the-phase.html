MONSTA X is returning to the K-pop scene. Their agency Starship Entertainment announced on August 7 that MONSTA X will make their comeback on September 4 with the mini album 'The Phase.'

MONSTA X surprised fans by dropping a coming-soon video for 'The Phase' through their official social media channels on August 6. The video opens with waves of light filling the screen, with the members depicted as particles of light — capturing the group's signature explosive energy and polished aura through dynamic performances, rushing movements, and trails of light spreading from their fingertips.

A standout moment in the video shows scattered particles of light gathering into one intense beam, completing the silhouettes of the members — a visual that hints at the new phase symbolized by the album title 'The Phase.' The album title and release date of September 4 are revealed at the end of the video, heightening anticipation for the comeback.

The coming-soon video is reminiscent of the VCR shown at the 2026 MONSTA X WORLD TOUR 'THE X : NEXUS' concerts. That VCR depicted the members crossing through a massive glowing portal to reach a new dimension. The new teaser appears to continue that narrative, drawing attention to what message and music MONSTA X will deliver through 'The Phase.'

'The Phase' is MONSTA X's first Korean release in approximately one year. With a deepened musical color, a solid team narrative, and high-quality performances that have earned them the reputation of a group worth trusting with your ears and eyes, they are expected to create another special moment with Monbebe (their official fan club name), who have long awaited this comeback.

MONSTA X has previously moved between solo, unit, and group activities, showcasing the varied musical textures that exist within the group. They also released their third full-length English-language album in the United States, expanding their global influence and drawing attention from major international media outlets. Through their world tour, they met Monbebe fans around the world, delivering powerful live performances and overwhelming stage presence at every show. With their North American 'THE X : NEXUS' tour continuing in October, anticipation is building for what music and performances MONSTA X will bring with 'The Phase.' Now celebrating their 11th debut anniversary, MONSTA X will release the mini album 'The Phase' on September 4 and kick off their full-scale promotions.
