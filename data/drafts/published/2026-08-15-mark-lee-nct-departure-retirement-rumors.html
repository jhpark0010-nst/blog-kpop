Mark Lee, who recently departed from the group NCT, has directly addressed retirement rumors that had been circulating among fans, and shared his plans for new music activities. On August 14, Mark posted a video titled 'Stories So Far and Dreams Ahead | ML@FL' on his YouTube channel 'Mark Lee.'

In the video, Mark reflected on the moment he wrote a message to fans ahead of his NCT departure. 'I thought a lot about how I should write it, and I even prayed,' he said. 'I thought putting my sincerity into it was the best I could do.'

He continued, 'I was truly grateful. I tried my hardest to write that the past 10 years were happy because of all of you, and that you are the reason I am here today.' With those words, he expressed his gratitude to the fans who had supported him for so long.

Mark also directly addressed the retirement speculation that had emerged from some corners following his NCT departure announcement. Recalling that period, he revealed, 'I don't think I even went home. My mom was honestly so scared.'

He then went on to clarify, saying people were asking whether he was retiring or becoming a private citizen.
