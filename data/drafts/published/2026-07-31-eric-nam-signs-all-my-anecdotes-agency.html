Singer and actor Eric Nam has announced a fresh start. He recently signed an exclusive contract with comprehensive entertainment company all my anecdotes (올마이애닉도츠).

Eric Nam has been consistently active in music throughout this year. He released the digital single 'How the Fire Started' on March 3, followed by his second single 'Miss Me More' on June 5. On the 29th, he previewed an unreleased track called 'Someday' via his personal SNS account, teasing its August 7 release date. He is also set to return on October 2 with his fourth full-length album, 'Confessions of a Lonely Heart.'

Additionally, he is scheduled to perform at the Grammy Museum Spotlight event in Los Angeles, USA on August 10. Eric Nam is also expanding his activities across broadcasting and film. He previously appeared on NBC Peacock's strategic survival program 'The Traitors' Season 4, finishing in third place overall. The show recorded the highest ratings of any season to date and has been nominated in six categories at the 2026 Emmy Awards, the most prestigious honor in American television.

Eric Nam also participated as a voice actor in the animated film 'Avatar Aang: The Last Airbender' alongside Steven Yeun and Dave Bautista. The film had its world premiere at San Diego Comic-Con on July 24 and began streaming on Paramount+ from July 25. He has also confirmed his appearance in 'K-Pop: The Debut,' a film co-produced by Paramount Pictures and HYBE America set for release in February 2027. He will star alongside actors Kang So-ra, Yoo Ji-tae, Yoo Ji-young, and Sung Joon.

Eric Nam's new agency, all my anecdotes, is a newly established entertainment company co-founded by Creative Director Lee Hae-in as CCO and former Warner Music Korea executive Kim Jay as CEO. Following the launch of virtual girl group OWIS, the company has also recruited Audrey, formerly of Jam Republic, and singer-songwriter DANY, and is currently preparing a new group.

Meanwhile, Eric Nam will release his new single 'Someday' across various online music platforms on August 7.
