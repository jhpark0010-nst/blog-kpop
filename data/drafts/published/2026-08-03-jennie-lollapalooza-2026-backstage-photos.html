BLACKPINK's Jennie has shared a series of behind-the-scenes photos from her Lollapalooza 2026 performance, showing off a striking presence in her backstage looks. On August 3, she posted multiple photos on her social media with the caption 'Lollapalooza 2026. Chicago.' In the images, Jennie is seen backstage wearing a feathered headband and radiating a one-of-a-kind aura.

Jennie drew particular attention for her bold outfit: a white military-style crop jacket worn over a daring underwear-style bra top. The combination of her dramatically exposed neckline, slim waist, and wild pants styling came together to showcase her signature daring fashion sense.

Jennie had previously taken the main stage as a headliner at Lollapalooza Chicago on August 1 (local time) at Grant Park in Chicago, making history as the first Korean female solo artist to do so. She performed a live band set of 18 songs over approximately one hour, including stages for new tracks. However, reactions were mixed among local media outlets, with differing views on the stage production and coverage from foreign press.
