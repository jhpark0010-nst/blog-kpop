ZEROBASEONE (Sung Hanbin, Kim Jiwoong, Seok Matthew, Kim Taerae, Park Gunwook) is proving its upward momentum with solid teamwork. The group recently won four awards at the 35th Seoul Music Awards and took home the 'ULTIMATE 12' category at the KMA (KM Chart Awards) 2026.

Cementing their status as a 'fifth-generation top-tier' act, ZEROBASEONE's sixth mini-album 'Ascend-' hit No. 1 on the Japan Oricon Weekly Western Music Album Chart, as well as No. 1 on Japan's iTunes Overall Album Chart and the K-POP and POP album charts. The album also reached No. 1 on Spotify's trending chart and No. 3 on the Billboard World Albums Chart, demonstrating the group's growing global influence.

Alongside the group's rise, the members' individual activities have also been drawing attention. Sung Hanbin, Kim Taerae, and Park Gunwook have been selected as MCs for various music programs and award shows, standing out with their steady hosting skills and sharp wit.

Kim Taerae showcased a different side of his musicality through a project commemorating the 50th debut anniversary of Sanulrim, drama OSTs, and cover content. Seok Matthew has been spreading his signature bright energy as a promotional ambassador for the Seoul Tourism Foundation's 'Visit Seoul' campaign and through his personal YouTube channel. Kim Jiwoong has been connecting with fans at home and abroad by holding fan meetings in South Korea and Japan.

Against this backdrop, ZEROBASEONE will release their 2nd Japanese EP 'Kaiki LOVE (回帰LOVE)' on August 19 and begin activities in Japan. Even amid busy group schedules, each member continues to express their individual personality and talent, proving the group's unlimited potential to shine in diverse ways.

ZEROBASEONE will kick off their Japan activities on the strength of what they have proven across multiple fields. Their new release — marking a Japan comeback after approximately 10 months — will be available on all major online music platforms at midnight on August 19. Meanwhile, it was in February that ZEROBASEONE decided to continue as a five-member group consisting of Sung Hanbin, Kim Jiwoong, Seok Matthew, Kim Taerae, and Park Gunwook.
