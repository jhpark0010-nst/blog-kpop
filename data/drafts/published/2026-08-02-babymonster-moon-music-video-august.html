BABYMONSTER will release the music video for 'MOON,' a track from their third mini-album, at midnight on August 3, according to their agency YG Entertainment on August 2.

'MOON' is a Southern Trap track — a subgenre of hip-hop — distinguished by its simple composition, blended with a mysterious synthesizer sound.

Since releasing their third mini-album 'CHOOM' in May, BABYMONSTER has unveiled three music videos in succession: the title track 'CHOOM,' the digital single 'SUGAR HONEY ICE TEA,' and the album track 'I LIKE IT.'

The 'MOON' music video draws viewers in with a dreamy atmosphere, a distinctive storyline, and bold direction. The BABYMONSTER members showcase a choreography performance that feels restrained yet brimming with confidence.

BABYMONSTER is currently on their second world tour, which kicked off in Seoul in June and spans Asia, Oceania, South America, North America, and Europe.
