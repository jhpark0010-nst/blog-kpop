Jimin (age 30) of BTS gave an unforgettable moment to a young fan with a serious illness, moving people around the world. On August 15 (local time), at the BTS WORLD TOUR 'ARIRANG' held in Arlington, Texas, Jimin gave a special gift to a girl named Olivia, who attended the concert as a guest of the Make-A-Wish Foundation, an organization that grants wishes to children with life-threatening conditions.

In footage that was released, Jimin can be seen handing Olivia a keyring and gently stroking her hair. The moment Jimin warmly hugged Olivia and kissed her on the cheek was captured on camera, drawing an outpouring of emotional reactions from fans.

Make-A-Wish shared on social media: 'We still can't get over this moment.' They added, 'At last night's concert, BTS gave our girl Olivia the sweetest hug. It became a precious memory she will never forget.'

Olivia, surprised by the kiss she received from Jimin, repeatedly exclaimed 'I got a kiss!' unable to hide her joy. After the video and photos were released, fans around the world responded with comments such as 'This will be a moment Olivia never forgets for the rest of her life' and 'You can feel Jimin's warm heart,' sharing in the emotion together.

Meanwhile, Jimin also drew attention on the day of Korea's Liberation Day (Gwangbokjeol) for his styling on the American stage, which evoked the Korean national flag, the Taegukgi. He performed wearing a hairstyle accented with red and blue hairpieces and an outfit reminiscent of the trigrams on the Taegukgi, and the concert staff also posted a photo of Jimin alongside a Taegukgi emoji.

Jimin had also previously made headlines last year when he wore a hat featuring a Taegukgi patch in the United States to commemorate the 80th anniversary of Korea's liberation.
