Stray Kids have wrapped up the Seoul leg of their new world tour 'RUN IT,' completing a total of five shows at KSPO Dome from July 25 to August 2, 2026. JYP Entertainment announced on August 3 that every single show sold out, reflecting the group's massive ambitions to expand into an even bigger world.

During the concerts, the members performed hit songs including 'God's Menu' (神메뉴), 'Hellevator,' 'Thunderous' (특), and 'Chk Chk Boom,' alongside previews of new tracks 'I Do' and 'After You' from their upcoming mini album 'THIS & THAT,' set for release on August 7.

Stray Kids shared their feelings about the performances, saying, 'We wanted to put on a spectacular stage that would blow away the summer heat. We prepared and ran toward this for a long time, confident it would be the greatest concert we've ever done, and the audience enjoyed it so fully that we were able to burn bright without holding back. We gained the strength to keep running without stopping, and we felt truly alive. We are sincerely grateful.'

The group also said, 'This concert is even more special because it holds the path we have walked as Stray Kids. Through the RUN IT tour, we want to show many people the road Stray Kids has run and will continue to run. We made a brilliant start thanks to all of you, who have been our compass guiding us in the right direction.'
