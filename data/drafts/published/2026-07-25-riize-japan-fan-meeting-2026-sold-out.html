Popular boy group RIIZE set hearts ablaze at their Japan fan meeting, drawing approximately 50,000 fans across six performances. 'RIIZE Japan Fan Meeting 2026 RPG - RIIZE Playing Game' was held at three venues: Fukuoka on July 10, Tokyo on July 15–16, and Hyogo on July 23–24. All shows sold out, and even restricted-view seats were opened to accommodate the overwhelming demand.

The fan meeting was planned around a game concept, and RIIZE engaged fans through dance and mission challenges as well as talk corners. They also performed 'KILL SHOT,' the ending theme song for the Japanese TV anime 'Kill Blue,' for the very first time on stage, and showcased songs including 'All of You' and 'Flashlight' from their second Japanese single.

RIIZE also performed 'Sunburst,' the title track of their third Japanese single set for release on August 26. The performance featured the members' free-spirited bouncing and sensuous footwork in sync with the track's breezy synths and rhythmic beats, drawing strong attention from the audience.

In connection with the upcoming third Japanese single, RIIZE will release the title track 'Sunburst' as a digital pre-release on various global music platforms at midnight on July 27.

RIIZE debuted in 2023 and has grown into a top-tier group within just three years. The group has proven their soaring popularity by setting the record for the fastest K-pop boy group to perform at Tokyo Dome.
