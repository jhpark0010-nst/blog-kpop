BIGBANG's new digital single 'BiiiG' has shot straight to No. 1 on major Korean music charts following its release, YG Entertainment announced on August 20. The track marks the group's first new music in four years and four months.

'BiiiG' was released at 6 p.m. on August 19 and reached the top of Melon's Top 100 chart — South Korea's largest music streaming platform — within just one hour. As of midnight on August 20, the song had also claimed the No. 1 spot on Bugs, Genie, and Vibe.

'BiiiG' is a high-energy (a genre of electronic dance music) hip-hop track featuring a grand beat and a highly addictive hook. Member G-Dragon participated in writing the lyrics and composing the music, deeply infusing the team's signature musical color.

The group is set to kick off their 20th debut anniversary world tour, 'XX : COSMOS,' starting in Goyang from August 21 to 23. The tour will span 33 shows across 19 cities worldwide, including stops in North America, Europe, Oceania, and Asia.
