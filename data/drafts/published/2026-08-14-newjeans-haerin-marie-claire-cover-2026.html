NewJeans member Haerin has taken part in a magazine cover shoot for the September 2026 issue of Marie Claire, marking her first individual activity since returning to her label. The magazine revealed the cover photos through its official social media accounts on August 14.

Marie Claire described the shoot as capturing 'a clear and vibrant moment of Haerin, like the beginning of a new season.' The magazine added that it 'caught Haerin's own face, where loveliness and confidence, elegance and freedom coexist, in a space reminiscent of a flower just blooming in an old Parisian garden.'

This is the first time Haerin has appeared as a magazine cover model since the February 2025 issue of Elle Japan.

It is also her first individual activity output since she and fellow member Hyein returned to ADOR in November 2025, following the exclusive contract dispute. The cover shoot was carried out as part of her activities as an ADOR artist.

ADOR, the agency behind NewJeans, explained that 'a proposal for the photo shoot came in, and it was arranged after discussing it with Haerin.' However, the shoot is reported to be unrelated to whether music activities will resume.

ADOR stated, 'We will inform you once the specific plans and methods for music activities are confirmed.'
