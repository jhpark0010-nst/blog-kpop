NCT 127 has kicked off promotions for their 7th full album with a concept centered on receiving a 'call' from fans. At midnight on August 3, the group opened the official promotional website for their 7th full album 'BLINGY.' With the album set for release on August 24, the promotion unfolds around the main slogan 'CALL 127.'

Through this concept, the storytelling presents NCT 127 as 'Seoul's problem-solvers' who gather in response to fans' calls, with members and fans forming a perfect 'one team' together.

The website is designed around a 'control center' concept where fans can check all online and offline promotions at a glance. Fans' visits to the site are framed as the act of calling NCT 127, and visitors can view behind-the-scenes teaser photos as well as listen to members' voices as if communicating directly with them.

On the same day, episodes 1 through 3 of the trailer series 'Call 127' were released on the NCT 127 YouTube channel. The videos show mysterious call signals being delivered to Haechan, Jaehyun, and Yuta as they go about their daily lives, drawing viewers' attention.
