Seoyeon, the younger sister of TWICE leader Jihyo, is set to make her official debut in the K-pop industry as a member of a new HYBE girl group. On August 3, ABD — a new label under HYBE Music Group — announced the formal debut of girl group TUIDE through its official accounts, releasing introduction photos and a video titled 'Introductions: WE TUIDE' showcasing the members.

TUIDE is a multinational girl group consisting of seven members: Seohee, Seoyeon, Elena, Jia, Saki, Sea, and Lee Haneui. ABD introduced the team by stating, 'The seven members, who come from diverse cultural backgrounds including Korea, the United States, the United Kingdom, and Japan, complete TUIDE's one-of-a-kind team color. All in their teens — born between 2006 and 2011 — they are set to bring a fresh kind of excitement to global music fans through their distinct individual personalities and multinational synergy.'

Among the members, Seoyeon has drawn intense attention from K-pop fans after being revealed as the youngest sister of TWICE's global top-group leader Jihyo. Born in 2008, Seoyeon has caught eyes with her striking features, which closely resemble those of her older sister Jihyo, and her polished visuals. Jihyo's second younger sister, Lee Haeum, has also already debuted as a model and actress, meaning that with Seoyeon now joining the music scene, all three sisters are active in the entertainment industry.

TUIDE will officially kick off their activities with the release of their first mini-album, 'TUNE & PLAY,' on August 24 at 6 p.m. The album, which carries the meaning of harmonizing different musical languages into a single rhythm to create joy, was produced under the overall supervision of Master Professional Han Seongsu, the former CEO of Pledis Entertainment. TUIDE also drew praise from industry insiders at a preview event held ahead of their debut, where they showcased impressive live vocals and performance skills.

Amid the news of her younger sister's debut, attention is also turning to Jihyo's own contract renewal situation. Jihyo recently shared her thoughts through a fan communication platform, saying, 'As the subject of my own life, I will think it over carefully and wisely and make my choice.' She also expressed, 'I sincerely love and cherish the team that is TWICE.' Her agency, JYP Entertainment, has stated that discussions regarding the contract renewal are continuing with careful consideration.
