ATEEZ has canceled their appearance at a K-pop festival scheduled to take place in Poland. Their agency, KQ Entertainment, announced on August 12: 'We would like to inform you that ATEEZ's appearance at SOUND IN COLORS × K-POP EXPOSÉ, which was set to be held in Poland, has been canceled.'

KQ Entertainment continued, 'We have been in ongoing discussions with the organizer to ensure the smooth running of the performance,' adding, 'We determined that it would be difficult to proceed with the scheduled performance as planned, as the organizer failed to fulfill their contractual obligations and other essential requirements for the event.'

The agency further stated, 'Taking into comprehensive consideration the smooth execution of the performance and the safety of our artists, we were compelled to make the unavoidable decision to cancel the appearance.' They also apologized, saying, 'We sincerely ask for the generous understanding of ATINY fans who have been looking forward to the concert, as we deliver this sudden and regrettable news.'

KQ Entertainment added that 'ticket refunds and related procedures will be guided through the organizer,' and asked fans to 'check the organizer's official announcements for further details.'

It has been reported that not only ATEEZ, but also STAYC, iKON, and THE WIND have canceled their appearances for the same reason. SOUND IN COLORS × K-POP EXPOSÉ was scheduled to be held on August 15 and 16 at Tauron Silesia Park in Poland, with a lineup that included ATEEZ, iKON, STAYC, MEIDIN, AT HEART, THE WIND, Taemin, and Suho, among many other artists. However, it has been reported that preparations for the event were disrupted by unresolved issues including the payment of remaining fees, flights, and accommodation.
