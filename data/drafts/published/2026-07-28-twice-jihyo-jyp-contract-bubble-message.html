Amid ongoing attention surrounding TWICE's contract renewals, member Jihyo personally shared her feelings with fans on July 28. Through the fan communication platform Bubble, she left a lengthy message for the fandom ONCE, opening up about her current state of mind. It had been a while since Jihyo directly communicated with fans, coming at a time when the entertainment industry has been closely watching whether TWICE members will renew their contracts.

Jihyo wrote: 'I could just have casual everyday conversations with ONCE, but my heart is still too heavy to chat so freely right now.' She added: 'I was so worried and cautious before reaching out, but I'll make sure to resolve and sort things out well so I can deliver news quickly without making ONCE wait too long.'

She continued: 'As the one in charge of my own life, I will think things through carefully and wisely and make my choice, so I hope ONCE will cheer me on and trust me like you always have.'

Jihyo also repeatedly emphasized her affection for the group. She wrote: 'I sincerely love and cherish the team called TWICE, and I believe ONCE will understand just from that feeling alone.' She closed her message by saying: 'I'm sorry it's been so long. I'll come back when I can have casual everyday conversations and crack jokes again. Sleep well, eat well, rest well, and let's see each other getting through it all. Thank you always.'

Earlier, reports had emerged that TWICE members Jeongyeon, Tzuyu, Chaeyoung, and Jihyo were linked to transfer rumors, with reports specifically stating that Jihyo was preparing to leave JYP Entertainment and establish her own one-person agency. In response, JYP Entertainment stated: 'We plan to provide an update once matters are confirmed,' declining to comment on specifics.

Jihyo debuted as TWICE's leader in 2015 and has been a core member of the group. She spent approximately 10 years as a trainee at JYP Entertainment before her debut, meaning she has been with the company for roughly 20 years in total. Last year, she participated in commemorative content marking her 20th anniversary with JYP. With her long-standing contract nearing its end, Jihyo's message to fans — that she will 'resolve and sort things out and deliver news quickly' — has drawn attention to her future plans and the direction of TWICE's activities going forward.
