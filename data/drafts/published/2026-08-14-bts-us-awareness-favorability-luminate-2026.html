BTS has had its US popularity confirmed in numbers, according to an announcement made on August 14 by their label Big Hit Music. The figures come from a Q2 2026 survey of US music consumers conducted by global music data company Luminate.

According to Luminate, BTS recorded a 42% awareness rate and a 46% favorability rate among US music consumers in the second quarter of this year.

For context, the average awareness rate across all artists surveyed by Luminate stands at 24%, meaning BTS scored 18 percentage points above that average.

BTS's favorability rating of 46% marks the highest figure recorded since Luminate first began tracking the group in 2021. Compared to the initial survey result of 34%, the favorability has risen by 12 percentage points.

Big Hit Music stated, 'The results of this Luminate survey show that BTS has built a broad public base in the United States that goes beyond fandom, supported by high awareness and favorability.'
