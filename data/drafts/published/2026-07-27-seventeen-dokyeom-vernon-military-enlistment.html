SEVENTEEN members Dokyeom and Vernon are set to fulfill their mandatory military service obligations. Pledis Entertainment announced on July 27 via official channels that Dokyeom will enlist in the army as an active-duty soldier on September 8, and that he will continue to participate in all scheduled official activities without disruption until his enlistment date.

Regarding Vernon, the agency stated that he will begin fulfilling his military duty on August 20, and will likewise participate in all scheduled official activities without disruption until that date. Vernon will serve as a social service worker (사회복무요원), an alternative form of mandatory military service in South Korea.

Once their service begins, Dokyeom and Vernon will be unable to participate in SEVENTEEN's group schedules. However, the agency noted that both members will continue to connect with fans — known as CARATs — through a variety of pre-prepared content.

SEVENTEEN had previously announced their second contract renewal with Pledis Entertainment. With military service obligations now affecting multiple members, the full 13-member group is currently unable to promote together as a complete unit.
