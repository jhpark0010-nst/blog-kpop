Girls' Generation member Kwon Yuri is leaving SM Entertainment after 19 years. On July 31, SM Entertainment released an official statement confirming that, following in-depth discussions with Kwon Yuri, both parties have mutually agreed to conclude their 19-year partnership as of August 31.

SM Entertainment stated: 'From Kwon Yuri's debut with Girls' Generation in 2007, leading the K-pop wave across the world and growing into a group that defined an era, to her shining as an actress — we are grateful to have been part of every step of that journey.'

The agency added: 'We extend our deepest gratitude to Kwon Yuri, who has shown remarkable activity as an all-rounder artist, crossing into diverse fields including music, acting, entertainment, and MC work.'

SM Entertainment also noted: 'Although her exclusive contract with us will come to an end, her activities as a member of Girls' Generation will continue unchanged. We will be cheering on Kwon Yuri as she embarks on a new journey.'

Meanwhile, Kwon Yuri is currently active as a member of the Girls' Generation unit HyoRiSoo, alongside fellow members Hyoyeon and Choi Sooyoung.

The following is the full text of SM Entertainment's official statement: 'Hello, this is SM Entertainment. We sincerely thank the fans who have consistently shown love and support for Kwon Yuri. After in-depth discussions with Kwon Yuri, we have mutually agreed to conclude our 19-year partnership as of August 31. From her debut with Girls' Generation in 2007, leading the global K-pop wave and growing into a group that defined an era, to shining as an actress — it has been a precious time to have accompanied her through every part of that journey. We extend our deepest gratitude to Kwon Yuri for her outstanding work as an all-rounder artist across music, acting, entertainment, and MC activities. Although her exclusive contract with us will end, her future activities as a member of Girls' Generation will continue unchanged. We will be cheering on Kwon Yuri as she walks her new path. Thank you.'
