NCT is collaborating with Seoul Fashion Week to celebrate the group's 10th anniversary since launch, SM Entertainment announced on August 13. The partnership will take place at Lotte World Tower in Songpa-gu, Seoul, the venue for the '2027 S/S Seoul Fashion Week,' and will include a stage costume exhibition, a runway show featuring NCT's performance outfits, and an NCT flea market.

The collaboration is a project that expands the musical world and concepts NCT has built over ten years into the realm of fashion. The costume exhibition runs from September 4 to 6, where fans can see performance outfits worn by each unit — NCT, NCT 127, NCT DREAM, WayV, and NCT WISH — all gathered in one place.

On September 6, a runway show will be held using actual stage costumes worn by NCT in their performances. Models will walk to NCT hit songs while wearing the outfits, presenting a new style of performance.

On the same day, SM Entertainment will hold a flea market at the Lotte World Tower lawn plaza, based on NCT's performance costumes and collectibles. All proceeds from sales will be donated to causes in the fields of women, youth, and culture.
