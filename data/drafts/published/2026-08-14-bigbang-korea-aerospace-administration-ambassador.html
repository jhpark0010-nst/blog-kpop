BIGBANG has been appointed as the inaugural honorary ambassador of the Korea Aerospace Administration (KASA), YG Entertainment announced on August 14, 2026. This marks the first ambassador appointment since KASA was launched in 2024.

As honorary ambassadors, BIGBANG will serve as a public communication partner, helping to introduce the future vision and key policies of Korea's aerospace sector to the general public in an accessible way, and joining the country's journey toward becoming a leading space power.

KASA stated, 'We appointed BIGBANG as our honorary ambassador in order to create new challenges in Korean aerospace together with the public.' The agency added, 'Going forward, we will work with BIGBANG through various communication activities to expand public interest and participation in aerospace.'

Meanwhile, BIGBANG is set to release a new digital single, 'BiiiG,' on August 19, and will embark on a world tour commemorating their 20th debut anniversary.
